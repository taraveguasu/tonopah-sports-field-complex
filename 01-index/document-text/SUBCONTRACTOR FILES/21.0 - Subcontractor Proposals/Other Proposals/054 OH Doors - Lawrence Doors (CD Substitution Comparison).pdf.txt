SERIES 650 651 652
Metal Curtain Rolling Counter Doors
The 650, 651 and 652 Series. A 
When your project requires a high-
quality, non-rated counter door sys-
tem that’s as handsome as it is
dependable, the 650/651/652 Series
will meet your specifications and
exceed all expectations. These 
precision-manufactured rolling count-
er doors feature a metal curtain in
your choice of three finishes — and
plenty more options that allow cus-
tomization to your specific 
application. Designed to fit openings
up to 18'4" wide by 8'4" high (5588
mm by 2540 mm), these durable
doors can withstand heavy usage 
or industrial environments, such as
stadium concession areas and parts-
storage areas. They are also stylish
enough for a variety of interior uses,
including cafeterias, pharmacies and
retail settings. 
The Choice of Steel, Stainless or Aluminum.
The 650/651/652 Series curtains are fabricated from
22-gauge galvanized steel (650 Series), 22-gauge
stainless steel (651 Series) or aluminum (652
Series) for handsomeness that holds up in nearly
any environment. Galvanized steel curtains are
factory pre-finished with a baked-on primer and
polyester coat to enhance finish durability and
minimize field painting. Guides are fabricated of extruded
aluminum with continuous silicon-treated strips standard.
The option of 10-gauge or higher roll-formed steel guide is
offered on 650 and 652 Series doors, and stainless steel
guides are available for the 651 Series.

Solid and Stylish Selection in Steel, Stainless or Aluminum.
Factory Pre-Finished for Longer Life.
Durable good looks are the hallmark of the 650/651/652 Series.
The 650’s galvanized steel curtain is factory pre-
finished with a baked-on primer and polyester coat to enhance
finish durability and minimize field painting. The standard fin-
ish is gray or tan, and a rainbow of powder-coat finishes —
including 21 standard colors, 172 premium colors, or color-
matched to your specifications — are available as options. The
651 Series’ s stainless curtains feature a standard No. 4 finish,
as well as an optional brushed finish. The aluminum slats of
the 652 Series are finished with clear anodization. Bronze
anodization is optional.
High-Performance, Low Maintenance. 
The 650/651/652 Series doors are constructed of high-quality
interlocking flat slats for a tight fit that is both sleek and
attractive. Endlocks secured to alternate slats maintain curtain
alignment and prevent lateral slat movement. The counter-
balance assembly features heavy-duty helical torsion springs in
a steel tube or pipe barrel to provide long and reliable service.
Optional crank operation or electric operation further simplifies
operation, and a 15-month warranty — as well as our network
of Ribbon distributors — ensures that your counter doors will
perform like new for years to come.
For additional information, or special project requirements, consult your Overhead Door Distributor or the Overhead Door Architectural Design Manual.

ARCHITECTURAL SPECIFICATIONS*
650  651  652
PART 1 GENERAL
1.01 Work Included
The rolling counter doors shall be Series 650, 651 or 652 as 
manufactured by Overhead Door Corporation.
1.02 Quality Assurance
In accordance with accepted quality assurance guidelines for motor-
operated doors, both the rolling counter door and electric operator
(optional) shall be provided by a single-source such as by Overhead
Door Corporation.
PART 2 PRODUCT
2.01 Curtain
Interlocking flat profile slats, type F-128, fabricated of galvanized
steel (650 Series); interlocking flat profile slats, type F-128, fabricated
of stainless steel (651 Series); interlocking flat profile slats, type 
F-128, fabricated of aluminum (652 Series). Endlocks shall be
attached to alternate slats to maintain curtain alignment and 
prevent lateral slat movement.
2.02 Guides
Guides shall be extruded aluminum shapes (Series 650, 651, 652)
with continuous silicon-treated strips (roll-formed steel shapes, 
10-gauge minimum (optional on Series 650, 652) (stainless steel
optional on Series 651).
2.03 Brackets
Brackets shall be steel plate to support the curtain, counterbalance
and hood. (Galvanized steel end covers on the Series 650, aluminum
end covers on the Series 652, and stainless steel end covers on the
Series 652 are optional). 
2.04 Counterbalance
Counterbalance shall be helical torsion springs housed in a steel
pipe barrel.
2.05 Hood
Hood shall be galvanized steel for the 650 Series; stainless steel for
the 651 Series; and aluminum for the 652 Series. Intermediate support
bracket shall be furnished to support hood where required. 
2.06 Finish
Aluminum material shall be clear anodized (bronze anodized).
Galvanized steel material shall receive a rust-inhibitive, roll-coating
process including baked-on prime paint to be .2 mils thick and
baked-on polyester top coat to be .6 mils thick. (OHD ColorSpec
optional powder coating finish.) Stainless steel material shall be 
No. 4 finish. All non-galvanized, exposed ferrous surfaces shall
receive one coat of rust-inhibitive primer.
2.07 Operation
Doors shall be manual push-up (optional crank or motor operation)
2.08 Locks
Locking shall be slide-bolt locks suitable for padlocks by others
(optional cylinder locks).
PART 3 EXECUTION
3.01 Execution
Install the rolling counter doors in accordance with Overhead Door
Corporation instructions and standards. Installation shall be by an
authorized Overhead Door Corporation representative.
