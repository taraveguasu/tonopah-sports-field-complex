Qualifications 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
- 
Excludes all material and hardware for openings 102B, 106A, 112 and 113. 
- 
Base total captures hardware as specified in the 087100 hardware sets. On the door schedule, door 106 is assigned remark 2 which indicates panic 
hardware. This opening is assigned hardware set 01 which does not have panic hardware. Add alternate 1 is to provide panic hardware at this door 
per remark 2. 
- 
While they are not specifically listed as a manufacturer in section 081113, hollow metal doors and frames are manufactured by Curries, an ASSA 
Abloy company, similar to Ceco Door. 
- 
Section 2.03, B. 2. calls for a minimum R-Value of 6.0. Exterior doors and frames will achieve an R-Value of 2.44 with a standard masonry frame and 
thermally-enhanced core. We have provided add alternate 2 to increase the R-Value for exterior doors and frames to 2.78 which is the highest R-
Value that can be achieved.  
- 
Excludes STC ratings per 056 scope of work item 1. b. ii. as there were no specific callouts for STC rated doors. 
- 
Base total excludes mock-up per 056 scope of work items 1. c. and 3. e. We have provided add alternate 3 to supply a door, frame, and hardware for 
mock-up. 
- 
Excludes attic stock per 056 scope of work item 3. a. Attic stock was not called out in section 087100.
