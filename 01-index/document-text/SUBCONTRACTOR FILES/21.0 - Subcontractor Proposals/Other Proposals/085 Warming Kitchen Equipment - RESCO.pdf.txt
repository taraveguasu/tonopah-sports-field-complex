Proposal Summary 
Total Proposal: $ 33,950 (applicable tax is included) 
 
Basis of Proposal 
This proposal is based solely upon the architectural drawings, equipment schedule, and limited food 
service information provided at time of bidding. No dedicated food service consultant drawings, utility 
rough-in drawings, coordination drawings, or written food service specifications were provided for this 
project. 
Pricing has therefore been prepared using industry-standard assumptions for a typical high school 
concessions installation. Any revisions, clarifications, additions, coordination requirements, utility 
requirements, code compliance modifications, or scope items identified after award shall be treated as 
additional work and addressed through change order. 
Value Engineering 
Our proposal is based on the contract documents as issued. RESCO can offer a cost-saving alternate 
whereby equipment is procured by RESCO and shipped directly to the project for installation by the 
General Contractor or others. 
This alternate can reduce overall project cost by eliminating field labor, warehousing, handling, and 
installation costs associated with a traditional furnish-and-install scope. 
Under this option, RESCO’s scope would be limited to equipment procurement and delivery coordination 
only. Installation, receiving, unloading, assembly, utility connections, and startup coordination would be by 
others. 
Pricing for this alternate assumes equipment is shipped direct from manufacturer to project site in 
standard manufacturer packaging and delivery conditions.

Equipment Scope Clarification 
The attached BID QUOTE represents the items included in this proposal and forms the basis of our pricing.

Scope of Work 
RESCO shall provide delivery to jobsite, uncrating, setting in place, and fastening equipment to walls and 
floors where specified. 
Clarifications and Exclusions 


 
Rough-in utilities or final plumbing, electrical, mechanical, or control connections. 
 
Building modifications, cutting, patching, coring, or fire/seal penetrations. 
 
Engineered or stamped drawings, delegated design, seismic engineering, or BIM modeling. 
 
Performance and payment bonds (available at additional cost). 
 
Subrogation waivers or insurance endorsements beyond standard coverage (at additional cost). 
 
Final cleaning, including removal of manufacturer protective coatings. 
Price Validity 
Pricing is valid for sixty (60) days from the date of this proposal unless otherwise stated. Absent execution 
of a written subcontract or Notice to Proceed within this period, pricing shall be subject to adjustment 
based upon manufacturer increases, material escalation, tariffs, freight adjustments, or market conditions. 
Schedule and Delay Escalation 
Pricing is based upon a reasonable and continuous construction schedule consistent with standard 
manufacturer lead times. Any delay, suspension, resequencing, or extension of the project schedule beyond 
originally anticipated installation dates shall entitle RESCO to equitable adjustment for storage, 
remobilization, additional handling, labor escalation, and manufacturer price increases. 
Payment Terms 
Proposal is expressly conditioned upon progress payments for equipment and materials upon receipt at 
RESCO’s warehouse. Stored materials shall remain insured and available for inspection. Final payment 
terms shall be net thirty (30) days unless otherwise agreed in writing. 
Warranty 
Manufacturer warranties apply. Warranty commencement shall be the earlier of substantial completion, 
first beneficial use, or sixty (60) days following delivery to jobsite.
