S T A N D A R D          C L A R I F I C A T I O N S: 
1. Concrete to be free of cures, retardants, or sealers, and to be a smooth hard troweled finish for polish and 
staining and a light broom finish for coatings. Floors should be protected prior to our arrival. Any stripping 
or clean-up required prior to the start of work, due to negligence of other trades (i.e. paint, drywall mud, 
etc) will be billed on a Time & Material basis, or through a signed Change Order. 
2. All areas are to be provided to NRC at the start of the job, in a clean, broom swept condition, scraped down 
and free of contaminants. 
3. Condition of the substrate is the responsibility of the General Contractor or Owner. 
4. Pricing is predicated on design, scope of work, quantities, and square footage, as well as consecutive 
workdays, uninterrupted by other trades. 
5. Repairs to existing cracks and/or imperfections in existing substrate prior to the application of coatings or 
topping is to help prevent such imperfections from showing through new finished flooring, however NRC 
cannot warranty or guarantee that such cracks and/or imperfections will not reflect through new finished 
flooring.  
6. The application of chemically reactive stains, solvent and water-base dyes on concrete surfaces creates an 
irregular, mottled finish, which varies greatly (in some cases dramatically), from area to area. Chemical 
stains and, or dyes will produce a wide variegation of color and not a one-color, monochromatic, paint-like 
effect. Irregularity in surface color is naturally inherent with these materials and should be expected and 
desired. 
7. Minimal space for storage of materials is to be provided by General Contractor or Owner. 
8. Crazing and pinholes are inherent to our topping products and will in no way affect the structural integrity 
of the material.  
9. Should concealed conditions be discovered, differing from those ordinarily encountered and generally 
recognized as inherent to the scope of work as identified in this proposal, the cost shall be adjusted through 
a signed Change Order or Time & Material arrangement. 
10. The above listed quantities stated herein are the basis for this cost proposal. Any dramatic increase or 
decrease to the footage will result in a Change Order, increasing or decreasing the contract amount. 
11. This project has been bid with fixed labor rates, and work is expected to occur during the regular “work 
week”, Monday through Friday 6:00 am to 4:30 pm, excluding holidays (unless noted otherwise). Should 
the construction schedule require unusual work hours, weekend or overtime hours, those additional costs 
will need written authorization from the General Contractor or Owner. 
12. Due to the liquid nature of the stains and, or dyes, sealers, coatings, desifiers & stain guards and it is 
recommended that our processes take place prior to any other finish material’s installation, (i.e. specialty 
finishes, base trim, cabinetry and toe kicks, natural stone, carpet or wood). Some of the chemicals utilized 
in our processes contain acids and metallic salts, coatings materials are corrosive to some materials and 
damage may occur if direct contact is made with these finishes. We will exercise our due diligence in 
protecting these finishes, but we do not include extreme protective measures other than our normal plastic 
and masking, and NRC will not be responsible for any damage that may result. 
13. This proposal excludes permit fees, bonding, traffic control, temporary utilities, (including climate control), 
moisture problems which may be inherent to the existing conditions. 
14. NRC requires full and unobstructed access to the work area for our men and equipment from start to 
completion.  If we are forced to clean up a work area in order to complete our scope of work above and 
beyond our contracted amount a change order will be issued.  Client is to also provide full access to our 
work area including but not limited to forklifts, elevators, ramps, and assistance in ingress and egress.  Any 
work associated with getting our men and equipment in and out of our designated work area in and above 
written authorization is subject to a change order.

17. Any artwork, samples, and procedures generated or designed through NRC or our employees, is the 
property of NRC and cannot be used without written consent. 
18. The overlay toppings and/or coatings used are not intended to correct existing problems that may be present 
in the substrate, (i.e. out of level, elevation problems, gaping structural cracks, efflorescence, moisture 
vapor emissions, moisture vapor transmission). Correcting these existing conditions will require change 
order. 
19. The architectural topping, resurfacing, or other systems on this project may include a sacrificial, 
maintenance sealer or stain guard which is used to aid in the clean ability of the surface, as well as offer 
protection to the surface. Upon project completion, NRC will provide the GC &/or Owner with and Care & 
Maintenance manual, which will give instructions on the cleaning and maintaining of the flooring or any 
scope of work we perform. After project acceptance, NRC will not be responsible for damage due to 
improper or lack of maintenance, misuse, nor neglect. 
20. The manufacturers of toppings, sealers, caulking products and grout, base their warrantees on the American 
Concrete Institute’s 28-day concrete curing period. We understand that this may not be conducive to fast-
track construction schedules and, in most instances, we have not experienced problems within a 14-day 
cure period. If the Owner or Contractor wishes to circumvent the recommended 28-day cure, it will be at 
his/ her discretion. NRC will not take responsibility for any problems that may occur because of inadequate 
cure time. 
21. Installation of toppings or coatings in adverse conditions such as low and high heat temperatures, high 
winds, humidity levels, high levels of moisture vapor, areas that have not been acclimated to manufactured 
required levels are not warranted by NRC.  
22. Protection of vertical walls and adjoining surfaces has been estimated with plastic at a maximum of 48" 
high. If a greater height of protection is required, a change order will be necessary. 
23. We will make every reasonable effort to protect areas and to contain the dust that may result from our 
placement, stamping, saw cutting, surface prep, polishing or decorative sand blasting, but we cannot accept 
responsibility for cleaning or dusting of surrounding areas due to ambient, airborne dust. 
24. If samples have not been submitted or approved prior to this proposal, certain assumptions have been made 
with regards to standard methodologies. If, through the sampling process, unusual or excessive 
methodologies are required, we will need to consider the cost impact and issue a Change Order if 
necessary. 
25. Our estimated costs in this proposal are based on the information and drawings provided. Should new 
construction drawings and specifications be provided that change our scope of work or an onsite inspection 
shows conditions differing from our original information a Change Order may be required. 
26. Certain topping systems cannot accept point loads prior to a complete cure. Any carts or heavy equipment 
must have pneumatic tires. Check tires for foreign objects such as nails or screws prior to their placement 
on the finished floor.  It is recommended that heavy objects, furniture, moving chairs, carts, etc. have felt 
pads or similar non-damaging protective devices affixed to the point of contact with the finished surfaces 
we provide. I.E. felt pads, non-marring plastic furniture leg attachments, moving heavy objects with heavy 
point loads with carts or other non-damaging means in lieu of dragging across finished surfaces without 
such methods.  Any damages caused by these means or similar are not covered by our warranty. 
27. Protection after our scope of work is recommended, but not always necessary. Floor protection is excluded 
from this proposal unless noted otherwise. Irregularities or damages caused by others during and after 
protection are not covered by our warranty and subject to a change order if requested to make repairs.  Care 
needs to be taken, taking into consideration cure times, covering materials, adhesives used and a variety of 
other protective measures unique to the project we install or work on.  
28. If water or solvent based dyes, as well as some chemically reactive stains and resinous systems are used in 
the coloration of this project, they may not be UV stable and may fade or experience a color shift when 
exposed to direct sunlight. The degree of fading depends on the color and the length and intensity of UV 
exposure. If UV is an issue we suggest chemical stains or water-based stains and urethane top coats which 
are UV stable.

29. Generally, the materials within the described scope of work require an ambient temperature of 60-85 
degrees fahrenheit to be installed. 
30. Areas of the building which open to the outside may cause excessive cracking of the topping products due 
to temperature change and or wind drying out the surface prematurely. We recommend these areas be 
closed off and sealed prior to our pour, otherwise NRC will not be responsible for any excessive cracking 
which may occur. 
31. Power required for shot blasting, grinding and polishing is 480-volt, 3 phase with 50 amp service and 230-
volt 3 phase and 1 phase with 50 amp service and is not included in this proposal, unless noted otherwise.  
32. Due to the nature of the chemicals proposed to protect the floor trowel marks, streaks, roller hairs and/or 
airborne particulate may appear in the finish. This is inherent with these products and will in no way affect 
the structural integrity of the material. 
33. Polished concrete flooring will not remove all existing stains in the concrete, they may be apparent in the 
finish. Polished concrete is susceptible to staining, especially if spills are left unattended, even for a short 
time. NRC is not responsible for removing existing stains or future stains nor can we warranty against 
staining that may occur. 
34. Due to the grinding process used to polish concrete or prepare it for other finishes various degrees of 
aggregate to concrete cream levels will be apparent. The floor will not have a uniform appearance due the 
natural variations of concrete this should be expected and desired, not all coatings and/or sealers may be 
thick enough to cover any and/or all shot-blast lines or grinder marks. 
35. Any and all warranties shall be void if coatings, toppings or concrete fail due to the presence of any type of 
treatment, penetrating sealer, contaminate, moisture, moisture vapor emission, moisture vapor transmission, 
alkali, efflorescence, substrate defects, misuse, abuse, or neglect and condition in the concrete such as ASR 
(alkali-silica reaction), AAR (alkali-aggregate reaction) and ACR (alkali-carbonate reaction).
