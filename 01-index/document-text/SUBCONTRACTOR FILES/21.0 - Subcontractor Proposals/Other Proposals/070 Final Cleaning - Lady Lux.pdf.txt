Proposal for Final Cleaning Services 
Project: NCSD Tonopah HS Sports Field Replacement – Concessions Building, Press Box & 
Ticket Booth​
Location: Tonopah, Nevada​
Prepared For:CORE Construction​
Bid Amount: $19,000.00 Lump Sum 
Scope of Work – Final Cleaning 
LadyLux™ Cleaning Company shall provide two mobilizations for final construction cleaning 
services associated with the new concessions/restroom building, press box, and ticket booth at 
the NCSD Tonopah High School Sports Field Replacement project. 
Construction Clean – Scheduled 8/27/27 
Construction cleaning shall include detailed removal of construction dust, debris, labels, 
adhesive residue, and trade-related buildup from interior surfaces throughout the concessions 
building, public restrooms, press box, and ticket booth. Scope includes cleaning of doors, 
frames, hardware, glazing, mirrors, counters, partitions, plumbing fixtures, restroom 
accessories, lockers, wall surfaces, equipment exteriors, lighting, diffusers, and accessible 
horizontal surfaces. Floors shall be vacuumed, swept, and damp mopped as appropriate to floor 
finish conditions. Interior and accessible exterior glass shall be cleaned. 
Final Clean – Scheduled 9/10/27 
Final cleaning shall include re-cleaning and detailing of all previously cleaned areas prior to 
owner turnover. Scope includes final dust removal, restroom detailing, glazing polish, touch-up 
cleaning from punch activity, fixture detailing, floor detailing, and final presentation cleaning 
throughout the concessions building, public restrooms, press box, and ticket booth to prepare 
the facility for occupancy and use.
