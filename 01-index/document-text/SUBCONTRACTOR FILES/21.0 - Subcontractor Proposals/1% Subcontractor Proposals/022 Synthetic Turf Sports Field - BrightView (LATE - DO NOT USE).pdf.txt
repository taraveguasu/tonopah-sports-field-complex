Qualifications & Exclusions 
Please note these qualifications and exclusions are to be incorporated into any contract issued. 
1. 
 General Qualifications 
1.1. This proposal is based upon Prevailing Rates 
1.2. The work outlined in this proposal shall be completed by 10/2027. In the event that the work is not 
completed by this date due to delays caused by scheduling issues beyond our control, the Client shall 
be responsible for additional labor and material costs incurred as a result of the delay.  
1.3. Tariffs 
• 
GTI has not included and reserves the right to add at a later time should they materialize, costs 
attributable to tariffs levied on goods from Canada, Mexico, China, or any other country subject to 
current or future tariffs. 
• 
Should any known or unknown tariffs be implemented after submission of this proposal and 
before project buyout that significantly impacts the price of any goods associated with the project, 
it shall be considered a change in scope.  GTI 1, Inc. will work with Core Construction to 
substantiate any increase with the use of vendor quotes. 
1.4. This proposal is valid for 60 days and pricing is subject to confirmation at time of award.   
1.5. Bid is based as an “all in” price with general conditions spread throughout. If portions of this proposal 
are accepted, we reserve the right to adjust our general conditions, as necessary. 
1.6. Overtime, night work, shift work and weekend work are all excluded.  
1.7. Dumpsters and portable toilets to be provided by the general contractor for our use. 
1.8. Changes in material availability may extend project schedule duration. Durations to be confirmed at 
the time of the contract. 
1.9. If the construction schedule or sequence is delayed or disrupted, through no fault of GTI 1, Inc., additional 
costs will apply. 
1.10. Temporary power and water during construction to be provided by others. 
1.11. GTI 1, Inc. will provide flaggers for its own operations as needed but traffic control and / or street & 
lane closures are to be provided and maintained by others. 
1.12. Permits, design, engineering, survey, audits, third party testing, inspections and licensing fees are 
excluded. 
1.13. Landscape maintenance is excluded 
1.14. Escalation has not been included.  
2.  Site Qualifications 
2.1. Site Shall be received: 
• 
Clean and free of grass, weeds, debris, and deleterious material  
• 
At +/- .10 foot of finish grade. 
• 
At +/- .10 foot of subgrade at hardscape areas. 
• 
All areas to be received graded to drain. 
• 
Rough grading is excluded. 
• 
Drainage and swales are excluded.
