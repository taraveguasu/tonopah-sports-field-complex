To Whom it May Concern, 
 
Advanced Welding & Steel wanted to reach out directly with an important and time-sensitive update 
regarding the U.S. wide flange beam market. 
 
Since the beginning of the year, wide flange beam pricing across the country has increased by more 
than 25%, driven by continued mill price increases, rising logistics and fuel costs, and tightening 
supply throughout both domestic and foreign markets. 
 
Several key factors are accelerating this shift: 
 
• 
Mill Price Increases: Multiple rounds of increases have already been implemented, with mills 
holding firm due to strong order backlogs 
• 
Logistics & Freight: Elevated fuel and transportation costs continue to materially impact 
delivered pricing 
• 
Supply Constraints: High demand—particularly from data center and infrastructure projects—
is limiting availability and extending lead times 
 
As a result, we are currently seeing little to no availability across many beam sizes, with lead times 
continuing to stretch—especially on larger sections, many of which are now pushed out to end of year 
or longer. 
 
In addition, many future mill rolling schedules are now listed as “Inquire” or have already closed, while 
upward pressure on pricing continues with no immediate relief in sight. 
 
For these reasons, we strongly recommend reviewing upcoming project requirements now. Securing 
material early will be critical in protecting both pricing and schedule, and taking delivery sooner rather 
than later may help lock in availability before further increases occur. 
 
Advanced Welding & Steel is actively positioning both domestic and foreign inventory to help 
minimize disruption through these market conditions. Our goal is to stay ahead of these shifts so we 
can continue delivering reliable service, material availability, and competitive solutions when they 
matter most. 
 
If you would like to review upcoming needs, discuss current availability, or secure future material, 
please reach out to our team directly. We appreciate your continued partnership and look forward to 
supporting your upcoming projects.
