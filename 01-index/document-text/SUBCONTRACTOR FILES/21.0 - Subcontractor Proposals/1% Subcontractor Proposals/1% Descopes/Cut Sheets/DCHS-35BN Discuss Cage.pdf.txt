The Jaypro Sports DCHS-35BN 6 Pole Discus Cage with Barrier Netting is a Professional Outdoor Discus
Cage System built for discus training. Its offset frame design minimizes ricochets, while individual hoist 
lines allow for quick installation and removal of nets. A durable, high-performance choice for schools, 
clubs, and training facilities.
 
System is comprised of 4” OD heavy duty aluminum curved poles and provides a 2' offset for added 
safety.  Each pole is equipped with a hoist line to make the net installation quick and easy.
 
When installing discus cage netting for the first time out of the box, it's important to understand 
that the netting may appear smaller or tighter than the actual listed dimensions. This is completely
normal and expected. The net needs time to relax and stretch into its true shape and size.
 
IMPORTANT NOTES:
 
1.
Before each use, check equipment for proper connecting hardware and structural integrity.  Replace 
any damaged or missing hardware immediately.
 
2.
Use of this equipment other than intended may be hazardous.
 
3.
Alteration or modification of this equipment mat be hazardous or result in injury.  For repair or 
replacement, contact your dealer or Jaypro Sports.
 
4.
The poles in the DCHS-35BN are intended to be permanently installed structures.  If ground sleeves 
are used, the poles can be removed at the end to the season if desired.  If this is intended, the 
ground sleeves should be properly covered to prevent any debris or water from entering the sleeve 
(Jaypro Ground Sleeve Covers EP0008-MOD).
 
5.
To increase the life of the netting, it is recommended to lower and remove the netting when not in use.
Avoid hanging additional equipment from the netting (banners, pitching targets, screens, etc.) directly
onto the netting.  If attaching anything to the netting, it is recommended to anchor it directly to the 
rope border, not the netting, or damage may occur and void the warranty.
WARNING:  OUTDOOR DISCUS CAGE USE DURING INCLEMENT WEATHER:
 
For your safety and to prevent damage to equipment, use of this discus cage is strictly
prohibited during inclement weather.  Do not use the cage during high winds or wind
advisories, during or nearby thunderstorms or lightning, during snow and/or ice storms
or any other severe weather warnings.  If winds are forecasted to be above 50mph, the
discus cage netting must be lowered to the ground or damage and / or injury may occur.
 
Jaypro Sports will not be held liable for any injury and/or damage that may occur if the
system is not protected against any inclement weather events.
 
When in doubt, stay out! Safety first!

ANNUAL MAINTENANCE / INSPECTION:
 
1.
Confirm all components / hardware are present and in good working order.  Inspect all bolted 
connections for tightness and replace any missing hardware or components.
 
2.
Clean any debris, leaves, etc. from the cage netting.  Remove any invasive vegetation if 
present.
 
3.
Inspect the area around the cage for any obstructions that may cause discus to ricochet off 
the netting and cause injury.  Clear the full perimeter within the poles and ensure clear 
walkways are outside the cage netting.
 
4.
Inspect all the poles for any defects and/or damage.  Should any defects or damage exist, 
contact Jaypro Sports and be prepared to send photos of the pole in question.  Do not use 
the system until proper review and assessment is complete.
 
5.
Inspect the area surrounding the cage system for drainage issues, erosion, and / or frost 
heaves.  Should any of these conditions exist, contact your local building code official for 
corrective action.
 
6.
Inspect the netting for broken mesh, cuts, pulls, fraying of material and discoloration 
indicating material aging.  Nets that show deterioration from mildew, wear, or stress that 
may affect the strength and performance must be removed from service for repair or 
replacement.
 
7.
At the end of each season, completely remove the netting for storage.  Take the proper 
means to protect the netting from rodent damage over the winter months or long periods of 
storage.  Store the netting in a dry environment.

7.  Insert a pole in each sleeve.
 
8.  Make up the ends of the cable as shown below. The cables should measure roughly 56' from thimble to
thimble.  When terminating the cable end, the saddle for the cable clamp should be on the live end of the 
cable, not the dead end cable tail.
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
9.  Roll out the net inside the frame and locate the ends.
 
10.  Lay the cable along the top outer rope border of the netting.   Attach a cable to both ends of the 
netting with the zip ties supplied, you may need to adjust where the intermediate hoisting ropes are 
attached.  Once secured, clip the tails off from the zip ties.
 
11.  Lower the hoisting ropes on the 2 end poles and attach the snap hooks directly to the cable thimbles 
at the end of the cables.  Evenly raise the net up to its maximum height.
 
12.  Lower the middle hoisting ropes and attach the spring clips to the cable directly in line with each pole. 
 
13.  Once net is fully raised, secure the bottom edge of the net to the ground anchor snap hooks.
14a.  Initial Set-Up:  Evenly raise the net to its maximum height.
Once the net is fully raised, secure the bottom edge of the net to
the ground anchor snap hooks. You may need to adjust where 
the intermediate hoisting ropes are attached, from one side of a 
zip tie to the other.  Adjust the position of the net so it is 
centered within the poles.  Double check that all the hoisting 
ropes are in line with the corresponding ground anchor.  With 
the net location and hoist connection points calibrated, lower the
net to about 4 ft above the ground and attach a D-ring at each 
intermediate attachment point, as shown below.  This will make 
future installation of the net a snap.  Attach the spring clip onto 
the D-ring.  Reconnect the hoist lines to the D-rings moving 
forward.
 
14b.  Routine Set-up:  Connect both end hoist cables to the 
cable thimbles.  Raise the netting up 4 to 5 feet above the 
ground.  Next, connect all the middle hoist cables to the D-rings.
Raise the netting to the desired height and secure the hoist rope
to the cleat. Once the net is fully raised, secure the bottom edge
of the net to the ground anchor snap hooks.
