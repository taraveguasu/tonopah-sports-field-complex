                Landscape and Irrigation Package #016 
 
Black Canyon Construction (BCC) landscape contracting company is pleased to provide this bid for the Landscaping 
and Irrigation scope of work based on plan pages L0.00-L0.02 (Tree protection ONLY) and L2.01-L2.03 and 
Specifications dated 4.20.2026 as well as Addenda 1 and Clarifications 1-2 for the NCSD Tonopah High School Sports 
Complex in Tonopah, Nevada. 
 
The scope of work includes labor, material, equipment, and related services for Landscape and Irrigation.  
BCC has included the following scope of work: 
1. Irrigation per plans.  
2. Temporary Tree Protection with temporary watering system prior to permanent irrigation installation. 
3. 40 hours for unforeseen irrigation repairs and/or temporary watering. 
4. Ground Treatments as noted in spec section 32 96 50 (Part 3.2-D Post Removal Application ONLY) 
5. Warranty 
 
The fee to provide the base scope of work defined above will be a total lump sum amount of One Hundred Eleven 
Thousand Two Hundred Seventy-Five Dollars ($111,275.00). Pricing is based on 2026 Clark County Prevailing Wage 
Rates plus Zone Rates, as determined by the Office of the Labor Commissioner (October 1, 2025), per project 
documents.  
 
The following are exclusions and/or clarifications to this scope of work: 
1. All other work not specifically listed above is excluded from our proposal. 
2. All Landscape Demolition is excluded and to be provided by others. 
3. BCC assumes that irrigation to existing plant materials is functional, reconnection of existing laterals only for 
existing irrigation. 
4. BCC has NOT included replacement of existing trees. 
5. Spec Section 32 96 50 is excluded in its entirety aside from notes above.  
6. All site grading (sub-grade prep) is excluded and to be provided by others, BCC has fine grade/finish for BCC 
scope of work only. 
7. BCC to receive the site free of construction debris and in finish sub-grade condition +/- 1/10.  All compaction 
of sub-grade to be provided by others. 
8. BCC is to have access to site prior to paving, concrete etc. to allow for installation of irrigation sleeves.  If 
sleeves are to be installed under existing BCC will not bear the cost of cutting, patching of asphalt and/or 
concrete, nor are any boring, jacking or jetting included.  
9. Any saw cutting, boring, jacking and/or drilling and/or patching of asphalt and/or concrete is excluded and to 
be provided by others. 
10. All rock areas to be received at depth to accommodate placement of rock. 
11. Any import/export is excluded and to be provided by others. 
12. All concrete, masonry, mow curb, flatwork, walkways and/or ramps is excluded and to be provided by others.  
13. All fencing is excluded and to be provided by others. 
14. All electrical/lighting/low voltage/telephone is excluded and to be provided by others. 
15. Hazardous material is excluded. 
16. On-site construction water and meter is excluded and to be provided by others. 
17. BCC will be responsible for Dust Control for BCC scope of work only. 
18. Survey, Potholing, Permits, Inspections, Quality Assurance/Quality Control, testing, and/or fees are excluded 
and are to be provided by others.
