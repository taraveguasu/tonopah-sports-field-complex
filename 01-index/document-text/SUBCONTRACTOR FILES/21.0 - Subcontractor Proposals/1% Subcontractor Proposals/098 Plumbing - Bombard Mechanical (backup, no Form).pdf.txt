PRECEDENCE 
 
The trade partner Proposer submitting on this Scope of Work Proposal Package acknowledges receipt and review of 
this document, understands the Work and Expectations set forth under this agreement, and agrees to the terms and 
conditions thereof.  This document is intended to compliment the Contract Documents, however, in the event of any 
inconsistency, the Contract Documents (Plans, Specifications, and Supplemental Documents), shall govern. 
 
SCHEDULE 
 
Refer to the published schedule with the Notice of Request for Proposals (RFP) for pertinent start, end, and critical 
milestone dates for work associated with this proposal package.  It is expected that the Proposer shall provide all 
necessary labor, material, and equipment to meet the published schedule durations as part of the Base (“Bid”) Proposal 
Value.  Should the Proposer feel that this published duration is not optimal and believe that a more cost-efficient 
duration option is available, CORE Construction (“Contractor”) encourages the Proposer to include this information and 
cost savings value as a Voluntary Alternate on the Proposal (“Bid”) Form. 
 
REASONABLE INFERENCE / GURANTEED MAXIMUM PRICE PROPOSAL 
 
This delivery method requires a Guaranteed Maximum Price (GMP).  No change orders will be issued for work that is 
clearly identified, or can be reasonably inferred from the Contract Documents (Plans, Specifications, and Supplemental 
Documents).  Reasonable inference is subject to industry best practice standards and experience.  If the Proposer is 
concerned with what may or may not be construed as Reasonable Inference, Contractor highly recommends issuing a 
Request for Information clarifying the matter.  Exclusion of this clause may be grounds for disqualification of the 
subcontractor’s proposal.   
 
EXISTING CONDITIONS 
 
The site is currently active and is not available to Proposers without coordination with Contractor.  Do not contact the 
client / end users for access to the site.  Contractor has published reference documentation on existing conditions of 
the proposed site at the FTP site.  These documents may include subsurface utility locating maps, ground penetrating 
radar maps, potholing reports, as-built documents, drone videos / images, laser scans, photos, etc.  If there is any 
specific information about existing conditions that is required to finalize the value of the scope of work proposal 
package, Contractor encourages issuing a Request for Information identifying the necessary data.   
 
If information that is needed to finalize the proposal value is unavailable at the deadline for proposals, include the value 
as an Allowance, clearly identified in the Voluntary Alternates section of the Proposal (“Bid”) Form.

1. 
Scope of Work – Plumbing – Provide all materials, labor, equipment, and supervision for a complete scope of 
work per plans and specifications. This scope of work shall include, but not be limited to: 
a. 
Disconnect water and sewer lines at existing concessions building, and salvage kitchen sinks, prior to 
start of demolition activities.  
i. Coordinate with Contractor for removal, protection, and storage of kitchen sinks to be re-
installed. 
b. 
Provide all labor, material, equipment, and components necessary for a complete and operable 
plumbing system, including drain, waste, and vent system, domestic hot and cold water piping, 
isolation valves, condensate drain piping (including for HVAC equipment, and pipe insulation), 
plumbing equipment (including electric tank water heater, expansion tank, recirculation pumps, 
TMVs, trap primers, backflow preventers, and water hammer arrestors), cleanouts, plumbing 
insulation, plumbing fixtures fittings, valves, supports, hangers, bracing, and accessories as required. 
i. Provide and install insulation on condensate drain piping. 
ii. Includes heat trace for water piping in unconditioned and exposed locations. 
c. 
Lay-out and verify all equipment pads, equipment supports, sleeves, openings, and block outs in 
structural roof and floor systems, masonry, and concrete, including sizes and locations. 
d. 
Supply and install all plumbing equipment, fixtures including floor and sink drains, hose bibbs, trim, 
sealants, and accessories for a completed plumbing system. 
i. Supply and install pipe protection at all sinks where pipe is exposed. 
ii. Install salvaged 3-compartment sink and hand sink at Concessions 106. 
e. 
Supply and install plumbing waste and vent systems – waste to 5' outside building line. Tie-in to site 
utilities if existing and provide flashings for all related penetrations.  
f. 
Supply and install drain pan under water heaters.

2. 
Scope of Work – General Scope Requirements – The following requirements shall apply to all Work under 
this Scope of Work package:  
a. 
Provide all tests and certifications required for complete and operational systems. 
b. 
Provide a qualified representative onsite for all tests and inspections as required. 
i. Subcontractor is responsible for scheduling testing and walking with inspectors to get 
systems signed off for final approval. 
c. 
Subcontractor responsible for scope coordination, including but not limited to: 
i. Coordinate with Contractor and the Masonry and Concrete Subcontractors for location and 
depths of all underground piping to minimize sleeving or stepped footings. 
ii. Coordinate with HVAC Subcontractor for all system interfaces, including sanitary vent 
locations with respect to HVAC equipment fresh air intakes, to ensure clearances are 
maintained. 
iii. Coordinate with Electrical Subcontractor for location of power to plumbing equipment and 
fixtures. 
d. 
Supply and install engineered seismic design, bracing, restraints, and supports for all equipment 
related to this scope of work as required and in accordance with the requirements of the project 
documents and the governing authorities.  
i. Comply with the requirements of the Geotechnical Report and Contract Documents for 
seismic requirements. In the event of a conflict in requirements between the documents, the 
most stringent requirement shall prevail.  
ii. All supports and hangers shall be coordinated with the architectural and structural 
requirements, and shall be attached to structural steel members. Piping and hangers shall 
not hang from decking. 
e. 
Supply and install all sleeves and flashings for this scope of work. Provide coordination drawings 
showing locations and sizes for all sleeved penetrations prior to starting work.  
i. Seal all through-wall penetrations related to this scope of work as indicated and, if rated 
construction, provide approved firestopping system in compliance with Contract Document 
requirements and the requirements of the governing authorities. Provide acoustical sealants 
for non-rated penetrations. 
f. 
Supply only all access doors and panels (required to be keyed a like), rated and non-rated as required, 
for access to plumbing work concealed by wall and/or ceiling construction.  Access doors and panels 
are required to be keyed alike and will be installed by others. Coordinate layout of backing, supports, 
access doors and panels with Framing Subcontractor. Coordinate type of access doors required with 
Architectural plans and specifications. 
g. 
Provide all trenching, shading, bedding, and compacted backfill of plumbing trenches. Haul off all 
spoils generated by this scope of work. Perform all hard digging, including Caliche excavation, haul 
and disposal, as indicated per the Geotechnical Report.

ALTERNATES 
 
The following alternates are identified in the Contract Documents or are Specifically Requested by Contractor through 
this Scope of Work Proposal Package. 
 
Alternate 098.01-A – (ADD) – Demo Wet Utilities at Abandoned Elementary School Campus  
State the amount to be added to Subcontractor's Proposal to demolish wet utilities at abandoned elementary school 
campus (Idaho Circle), as indicated and required, including: 
 
a. 
Cut and cap water and sewer lines scheduled for demolition, including sawcutting of slab-on-grade, 
excavation, backfill, and concrete patch back per detail 3/AD1.15. Remove existing plumbing fixtures as 
indicated for haul off by others.  
b. 
Cut and cap existing propane piping (flush at top of slab at buildings to be demolished, and above roof line 
at buildings / structures to remain, as indicated). 
c. 
Disconnect existing propane tank, for removal by others, including cut and cap of propane piping as required.

State the amount to be added to Subcontractor's Proposal to supply and install new propane tank at abandoned 
elementary school campus (Idaho Circle), as indicated and required, including concrete pad, piping to point of 
connection, and all incidental materials and equipment as required for a complete scope of work.
