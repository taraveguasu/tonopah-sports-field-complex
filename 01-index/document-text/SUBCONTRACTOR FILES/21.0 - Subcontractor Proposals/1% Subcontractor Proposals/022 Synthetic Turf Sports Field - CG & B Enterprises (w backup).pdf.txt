BASE SCOPE PRICING INCLUDES THE FOLLOWING: 
1) 
Bid acknowledges Addendum 1 and Clarifications 1 & 2. 
2) 
This pricing is provided to supply supervision, labor and material for the installation of the synthetic turf system and nailer board package 
for the Tonopah High School project per request. The complete scope of work is listed below; no other work is included in the total 
pricing unless specifically listed. 
a. 
Supply and install 4” of new base material & provide fine grading to achieve proper planarity per manufacturer standards for 
new synthetic turf system. NOTE: Base Scope budget pricing expects subgrade elevation to be left at FG less the turf system 
and the 4” of base material. There is no pricing in the Base Scope portion of this budget pricing to remove any existing 
material or for bringing in new or additional subgrade material. 
b. 
Supply & install approximately 1,134 LF of nailer board on concrete curb (PCC curb supplied by others).  
c. 
Supply & install approximately 73,578 square feet of SOTERIA™ 20 mm shock pad. 
d. 
Supply & install approximately 73,578 square feet of Hellas MATRIX HELIX 46 oz. 100% polyethylene twisted monofilament 
with Shape Memory Technology synthetic turf system with the noted installation options listed below: 
i. Natural ELIA RENUFILL & REALFILL pea gravel infill provided/installed at manufacturers approved infill rates per SF.  
ii. Supply GMAX & HIC testing. 
iii. Supply standard 8-year manufacturer warranty supported by a third party insurance policy for the synthetic turf 
system upon project completion per project specifications.  
e. 
Supply an operation and maintenance orientation for care of the synthetic turf field system upon project completion. 
3) 
Pricing is based on a standard, 8-hour work shift (normal working hours) at current published Prevailing Wage decision. No overtime, 
shift, holiday, or weekend pay is included. Union wages are not included and CG&B is not signatory to any union agreements or PLAs for 
this project. 
4) 
One mobilization/demobilization is included in pricing. Anything past that will be charged at $6,500 per mobilization.
