CONDITIONS 
 
1. This proposal is based on NorthStar being awarded all work under one contract. Price

proposal. 
4. Sewer lines will be capped at project limits. The owner/GC shall be responsible for the closeout 
and termination of utility lines that will be affected by this work. 
5. Tree removal is limited to stump removal of up to 24 inches below adjacent grade; removal of 
the root system is not part of this proposal. 
6. The quoted price reflects that all work will be done between normal working hours.  
7. This proposal is based on one mobilization.  Additional mobilizations are $3,500.00 each.   
8. All work is prevailing wage.

NorthStar CGL Insurance.

14. All salvage belongs to NorthStar Contracting Group, Inc. unless noted otherwise on plans or 
specs.

encountered NorthStar can remove at an additional cost.
