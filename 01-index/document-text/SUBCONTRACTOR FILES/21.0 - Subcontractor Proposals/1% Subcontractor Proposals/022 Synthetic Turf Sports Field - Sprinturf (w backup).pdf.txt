HISTORY - 100% AMERICAN MADE & OWNED 
Founded in 1998, Sprinturf is one of the original pioneers in the artiﬁcial turf 
industry, with over 3,500 installations across the United States. In 2010, Sprinturf 
merged with Integrated Turf Solutions (ITS), one of only two companies 
manufacturing artiﬁcial turf in North America. This partnership created the new 
Sprinturf, making it the ﬁrst fully-integrated polyethylene turf company in North 
America. Today, Sprinturf stands as a world-class leader, delivering unmatched 
economics, logistics, quality, and service. 
LOCATIONS 
Sprinturf's headquarters are located in Charleston, South Carolina. The company 
operates two manufacturing facilities — one in Chatsworth, Georgia, and the 
other in Dadeville, Alabama. The Dadeville facility specializes in extruding a wide 
range of polyethylene, polypropylene, and nylon yarns, including the latest slit-
ﬁlm and spinneret monoﬁlament yarns. The tufting and urethane coating 
processes are carried out at the Chatsworth facility. 
CAPABILITIES 
At Sprinturf, we take pride in completing projects on time, consistently 
outperforming the industry average. Within just 21 days of receiving approved 
submittals, Sprinturf can manufacture, deliver and install synthetic turf for your 
project. We provide an 8-year manufacturer’s warranty, insured by a third party, 
that guarantees our warranty. Sprinturf compounds our own urethane allowing 
us to provide industry leading tuft bind. Throughout our entire history, not a 
single ﬁber produced in our facilities has ever failed — a true testament to the 
superior quality of our products. 
Additionally, Sprinturf offers SprintCare, a unique maintenance package 
designed to support you throughout the life of your ﬁeld. From the initial stages 
of manufacturing to the eventual removal and replacement when your ﬁeld 
reaches the end of its lifespan, Sprinturf is with you every step of the way. 
We provide competitive pricing while ensuring all production complies with EPA 
and CPSC regulations. 
QUALITY 
The Sprinturf system stands out in numerous ways. Because we handle all 
production in-house, our quality control focuses on prevention rather than 
detection. Our manufacturing facility is ISO-9001 certiﬁed, and we produce all 
ﬁbers domestically in the U.S. We’ve been producing slit tape ﬁbers since 2002 
and spinneret monoﬁlament since 2006. By avoiding imports and having a single 
source for ﬁbers, Sprinturf not only ensures superior quality control but also 
maintains full compliance with U.S. laws and regulations. From raw materials to 
the ﬁnished product, we oversee every aspect of manufacturing, including tufting 
and urethane coating. Additionally, our in-house lab is equipped with advanced 
tools like Instrons, spectrophotometers, UV weatherometers, and a Lisport 
accelerated wear tester to guarantee the highest standards.

MANUFACTURING 
Sprinturf produces top-tier turf systems through its vertically integrated 
manufacturing process and unmatched quality control. With cutting-edge 
manufacturing equipment at our facilities in Georgia and Alabama, we lead the 
industry in yarn production. Our state-of-the-art extrusion lines create 
UltraBlade and MX ﬁbers, featuring the latest monoﬁlament and slit tape 
technology. We extrude a variety of yarns—nylon, polyethylene, textured, dual-
colored, and dual-shaped — using only premium resins and color concentrates. 
Our ISO-9001 certiﬁed tufting and coating facility, in operation since 2004, 
manufactures a wide range of artiﬁcial turf products for landscapes and sports 
ﬁelds. Our proprietary urethane formulas provide superior tuft binds, and our 
custom perforation system offers enhanced drainage, exceeding 60 inches per 
hour without inﬁll. 
AMERICAN-MADE 
Sprinturf is deeply committed to investing in the United States and 
American workers. We are the only turf manufacturer that fully 
produces all of our turf systems in-house within the U.S., from 
pellet to play. Every job and every dollar spent stays in the country. 
SAFETY 
Sprinturf offers some of the safest playing surfaces available. Our patented 
100% all-rubber system, along with standard rubber and sand mix options, 
ensures player safety. Our GreenPlay EVO inﬁll, made from 100% coconut 
husk, provides an organic, high-performance alternative that mimics natural 
grass temperatures, signiﬁcantly reducing surface heat. This allows athletes to 
perform longer with less fatigue, while the system’s stability minimizes shock 
and impact on players' joints. We only use polymers from trusted suppliers like 
Dow and Exxon, and all materials comply with California's stringent 
Proposition 65 standards for safety and environmental regulations. 
FINANCIALS AND BONDING
As one of just four national synthetic grass companies, Sprinturf owns all of 
its U.S.-based manufacturing facilities. We maintain long-standing 
partnerships with leading resin suppliers Exxon and Dow, ensuring a steady 
supply for our 50-million-square-foot annual manufacturing capacity. Sprinturf 
carries comprehensive insurance, including general liability, auto liability, and 
workers' compensation. We offer an industry-leading third-party insured 
warranty, covering up to $10 million per ﬁeld and a $10 million annual 
aggregate. Our surety bond provider is prepared to support contract 
performance and payment bonds, with an aggregate program limit of $75 
million, and is open to considering higher limits if needed due to our ﬁnancial 
strength and industry expertise. As part of the PlayCore family, Sprinturf 
leverages industry-leading research and expertise to deliver even greater value to 
its customers. PlayCore's commitment to safety, community health, and 
environmental stewardship ensures that Sprinturf's systems not only meet the 
highest performance standards but are also designed with a holistic focus on 
long-term sustainability and playability.

ISO-9001 CERTIFICATION 
ISO-9001 is the international standard for Quality Management Systems (QMS), 
published by ISO (the International Organization for Standardization). In order to 
be released and updated, ISO-9001 had to be agreed upon by a majority of 
member countries so that it would become an internationally recognized 
standard, which means it is accepted by a majority of countries worldwide. 
 
QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (QMS) 
The Quality Management System, which is often referred to as a QMS, is a 
collection of policies, processes, documented procedures and records. This 
collection of documentation deﬁnes the set of internal rules that will govern how 
your company creates and delivers your product or service to your customers. The 
QMS must be tailored to the needs of your company and the product or service 
you provide, but the ISO-9001 standard provides a set of guidelines to help make 
sure that you do not miss any important elements that a QMS needs to be 
successful. 
 
ISO-9001 CERTIFICATION BENEFITS 
The ISO-9001 standard is recognized worldwide 
and you can be conﬁdent that Sprinturf: 
• Minimizes mistakes 
• Improves reporting and communications 
• Provides better quality products and service 
• Has reliable production scheduling and delivery 
• Standards are maintained by annual assessments
