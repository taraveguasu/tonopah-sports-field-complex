REASONABLE INFERENCE / GURANTEED MAXIMUM PRICE PROPOSAL

This delivery method requires a Guaranteed Maximum Price (GMP).  No change orders will be issued for work that is clearly identified, or can be reasonably inferred from the Contract Documents (Plans, Specifications, and Supplemental Documents).  Reasonable inference is subject to industry best practice standards and experience.  If the Proposer is concerned with what may or may not be construed as Reasonable Inference, Contractor highly recommends issuing a Request for Information clarifying the matter.  Exclusion of this clause may be grounds for disqualification of the subcontractor’s proposal.

If information that is needed to finalize the proposal value is unavailable at the deadline for proposals, include the value as an Allowance, clearly identified in the Voluntary Alternates section of the Proposal (“Bid”) Form.

Scope of Work – Lockers – Provide all materials, labor, equipment, and supervision for a complete scope of work per plans and specifications. This scope of work shall include, but not be limited to:
Supply and install all heavy-duty single-tiered and three-tiered metal Lockers, including all shelves, rods, hooks, ventilation louvers and finishes, as indicated and specified.
Coordinate with Framing Subcontractor for installation of locker supports.
Provide all adhesives, profiles, etching, anchors, fasteners, and final adjustments.
Furnish and install number plates and locking devices as specified.
Provide all adhesives, profiles, etching, anchors, fasteners, and final adjustments.
Subcontractor shall receive, offload, inventory, and store materials when delivered to jobsite.
Provide a qualified representative onsite for all inspections as required.
Provide Shops Drawings for and coordinate layout with the Contract Documents and comply with all applicable ADA guidelines.
All materials to comply with flame spread ratings as required; provide manufacturers certifications.
Installation and materials shall comply with all applicable governing codes regulations and guidelines.
Coordinate with Contractor and Framing Subcontractor for all backing and blocking requirements for this scope of work.
Make final adjustments at completion of this scope of work as required for an acceptable system.
Include multiple mobilizations as may be required per the Preliminary Construction Schedule and Logistics Plan.
