REASONABLE INFERENCE / GURANTEED MAXIMUM PRICE PROPOSAL

This delivery method requires a Guaranteed Maximum Price (GMP).  No change orders will be issued for work that is clearly identified, or can be reasonably inferred from the Contract Documents (Plans, Specifications, and Supplemental Documents).  Reasonable inference is subject to industry best practice standards and experience.  If the Proposer is concerned with what may or may not be construed as Reasonable Inference, Contractor highly recommends issuing a Request for Information clarifying the matter.  Exclusion of this clause may be grounds for disqualification of the subcontractor’s proposal.

If information that is needed to finalize the proposal value is unavailable at the deadline for proposals, include the value as an Allowance, clearly identified in the Voluntary Alternates section of the Proposal (“Bid”) Form.

Scope of Work – Scoreboards – Provide all materials, labor, equipment, and supervision for a complete scope of work per plans and specifications. This scope of work shall include, but not be limited to:
Supply and install Scoreboards, complete with all steel support structures as required.
Provide scoring console, electric operators, and operating hardware as specified and required.
Subcontractor shall employ a licensed electrician to complete all electrical work related to the equipment, other than final connections indicated on the electrical plans.
Supply and install all structural components, including support posts and framing, welding, priming, and prefinished items as specified.
Coordinate with Electrical Subcontractor for load requirements of transformer and enclosure to be mounted behind scoreboard. See sheet note 3/E1.10. Supply and installation of transformer and enclosure, and final connections to transformer, by Electrical Subcontractor.
Supply and install footings including excavation, backfill, compacted subbase, concrete, reinforcing, sleeves, anchors, coring if required, layout, and clean-up for these scopes of work.  Backfill all footing excavations.
Provide all deferred submittals, including engineering and permits, as required for this scope of work.
Structural foundation and framing design provided in Bid Documents is for reference only. Subcontractor shall provide all design and engineering required for a complete turnkey installation of scoreboards, including all associated steel structure / framing, and foundations / footings.
Provide all anchors, fasteners, and sealants required to provide a complete scope of work.
Provide final adjustments, start up, and training as specified.
Materials and installation shall comply with applicable ADA requirements.
Coordinate with Contractor for location of all underground piping prior to installation of foundations.
Provide dust control for all operations related to this scope of work during working hours per Nye County Air Quality requirements.
Remove and legally dispose of all dirt spoils generated by this scope of work.
Include multiple mobilizations as may be required per the Preliminary Construction Schedule and Logistics Plan.
Subcontractor shall provide competent foreman who shall participate in daily huddle and weekly workplan meetings.
