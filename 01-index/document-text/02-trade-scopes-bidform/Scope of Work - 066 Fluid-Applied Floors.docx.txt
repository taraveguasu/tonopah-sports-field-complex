Scope of Work – Fluid-Applied Flooring – Provide all materials, labor, equipment, and supervision for a complete scope of work per plans and specifications. This scope of work shall include, but not be limited to:
Floor preparation prior to the installation of resinous flooring with integral cove base shall be performed in accordance with manufacturer’s recommendations, including moisture testing; mechanical treatment; crack and joint cleaning; patching; and key cutting, as required for a complete scope of work.
Supply and install all Fluid-Applied Flooring as specified for each application.
Provide resinous flooring and integral cove base as indicated.
Provide all color-fast flakes / chips as indicated and specified.
Supply and install metal termination trim / strip at top of coved base, whether shown or not on drawings.
Supply and install floor transitions at adjacent flooring base, whether shown or not on drawings.
Fill all concrete sawcut joints with mesh to prevent cracking.
Furnish and install material for mockup. Coordinate with onsite Superintendent for mock-up size and requirements.
Clean and protect finished surfaces with fully taped ram-board (Skudo, or similar) for this scope of work upon completion.
Resinous Flooring Manufacturer (Manufacturer) shall provide Resinous Flooring System (System) warranty as specified.
Provide all tests and certifications required for complete and operational systems.
Provide a qualified representative onsite for all tests and inspections as required.
Subcontractor is responsible for scheduling testing and walking with inspectors to get systems signed off for final approval.
Provide all layout related to this scope of work as required.
All materials to comply with flame spread rating as required; provide manufacturers certifications.
Submit product information including samples of materials and colors to be selected by the Owner.
Subcontractor shall inspect finished concrete subfloor ahead of installation activities, including inspecting for proper tolerances. Report any discrepancies to Contractor.
Perform floor preparation including any required fillers and sanding as per flooring manufacturer’s recommendations. Scope of work of this Subcontract does not include floor repairs.
Provide concrete floor moisture testing required by the Contract Documents and/or manufacturer's recommendations.
Receive, offload, inventory, handle, hoist, place, and store materials and equipment necessary for the execution of this scope of work.
Include multiple mobilizations as may be required per the Preliminary Construction Schedule and Logistics Plan.
