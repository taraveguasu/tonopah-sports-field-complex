DESCRIPTION OF THE PROJECT 
 
The overall NCSD Tonopah High School Sports Complex project consists of: 
 
Selective site demolition including existing running track surfacing, synthetic turf sports field, fencing and gates, 
grandstands/bleachers, press box, and concessions building. Salvage of select existing equipment is required, 
including sinks at concessions kitchen, flag, irrigation equipment, and A/V equipment. 
 
New site work consists of earthwork; asphalt paving; wet and dry utilities; concrete flatwork; irrigation; synthetic turf 
sports field; running track surfacing; storm drainage system including linear drains at inside track curb; fencing & 
gates, including discuss cage; exterior athletic (Track & Field) equipment; grandstands with integral walking platform, 
stairs, and accessible ramp; press box; scoreboard; prefabricated ticket booth; site furnishings; sports field lighting; 
and flagpole. Waterproofing/damproofing, site caulking and sealants, and site painting are required.  
 
New Concessions building construction consists of structural concrete foundations & slab-on-grade; masonry; steel 
joists & decking; metal roofing & fascia panels; overhead coiling doors; hollow metal doors, frames & hardware; 
framing & drywall; insulation; acoustical ceilings; painting & coatings; epoxy flooring; finished concrete; (warming 
kitchen) food service equipment; lockers; marker boards & menu display case; signage; restroom & fire protection 
specialties; and Plumbing, Mechanical, Electrical & Low Voltage systems. Electrical and Low Voltage systems to be 
tied back to existing High School ER/TR. 
 
The overall NCSD Tonopah Elementary School Demolition work (also under this project) consists of: 
 
Demolition at abandoned elementary school campus includes hazardous materials abatement & full building 
wrecking of abandoned school buildings at a remote location, located at 1220 Idaho Circle, Tonopah, NV. Safe-off 
and demolition of existing secondary power feeders; demolition and replacement of existing propane tank; 
demolition of select site finishes and amenities; and new fencing and gate are also required at this site. Excavate 
below grade, cut, cap & bury existing wet utility stub ups at existing slabs-on-grade to remain. Salvage and reinstall 
existing exterior canopy columns, including new connections to existing canopies to remain. Demo existing trees and 
shrubs, including stump grinding. Cut and cap existing water and propane piping. 
 
The total construction budget is estimated at $12,000,000.

NEVADA REVISED STATUTES*

Notice: For a proposal for a subcontract on the public work to be considered: 
 
1. 
The subcontractor must be licensed pursuant to Chapter 624 of NRS; 
2. 
The proposal must be submitted on the form provided by the construction manager at risk and be 
timely received; 
3. 
If a preproposal meeting regarding the scope of the work to be performed by the subcontractor is held, 
the subcontractor must attend the preproposal meeting; and 
4. 
The subcontractor may not modify the proposal after the date and time the proposal is received. 
 
Paragraph 6 states that to be considered responsive, a proposal must: 
 
a) 
Be submitted on the form provided by the construction manager at risk pursuant to subsection 3; 
b) 
Be timely received by the construction manager at risk; and 
c) 
Substantially and materially conform to the details and requirements included in the proposal 
instructions and for the finalized bid package for the public work, including without limitation, details 
and requirements affecting price and performance.
