## Root causes

### RC1 — The 33 Scope of Work narratives were never read
`package-index.json` stored `scope_of_work_doc` as a bare filename. Nothing opened the files.
They extract cleanly: **362,376 characters across 35 documents**. These are the most
authoritative statement of what each package carries, and they resolve boundaries the
drawings leave ambiguous. Two questions the index flagged as *"no source doc resolves this"*
are answered verbatim in documents that were sitting in the repo unread:

- **Demo boundary** (flagged unresolvable) — SOW-008: *"Demolish existing athletic equipment,
  including foundations / footings... Football goal posts. Track & field equipment as
  indicated, including landing pit materials... Demolish, remove, and dispose of all existing
  turf materials."*
- **RFP-002/RFP-008 salvage overlap** (flagged as a new finding) — SOW-008: *"Demolish and
  remove from site existing Modular Concessions Building, including foundations"* and
  *"Salvage and turnover to Contractor items scheduled to be salvaged."*

### RC2 — Addenda were collected but never applied as supersession
Addendum #1 revised **7 sheets**: G0-00, LS1-10, A1-20, A2-10, A10-30 (architectural) and
C1, GD (civil). The vision pass indexed the base bid set exclusively; the revised sheets in
`06-addenda/` were never opened. **16 of 33 packages** cite a base-bid sheet that ADD 1
superseded:

| Superseded sheet | Packages citing the dead version |
|---|---|
| A1-20 | ITB-018, ITB-078, ITB-089, RFP-023, RFP-030, RFP-094, RFP-109 |
| GD | RFP-008, RFP-021, RFP-022 |
| A10-30 | ITB-071, ITB-077 |
| A2-10 | ITB-062, RFP-060 |
| G0-00 | ITB-089, RFP-094 |
| LS1-10 | ITB-074, RFP-045 |
| C1 | RFP-008 |

Worse than a collection gap: for ITB-089 the index *already held* the superseding fact in
`addenda_refs` (*"RFI #5 Scoreboard model FB-2022 discontinued; Basis of Design changed to
FB-2021"*) while its `drawing_sheets` entry asserted Watchfire. A direct contradiction inside
one package record, unflagged. There is no revision field anywhere in the schema.

### RC3 — Awarded subcontractor proposals were mapped but not read
`awarded_sub.proposal_file` is a path. Nothing extracted inclusions, exclusions, or
clarifications from the proposals or the descope agendas. The 07.10.26 decision to draft each
1% package *reconciled to the awarded sub* was never executed at index level.

### RC4 — Trade-boundary rules are not encoded
Assignments were made from what a sheet depicts, not from which package's scope doc claims it.
Hence self-adhered roofing membrane → ITB-040 instead of RFP-045, and concrete curb / trench
drains missing from RFP-030.

## What was and wasn't wrong with the vision pass

Precision on *what it read* held up: A7-10 and A10-30 matched their sheets verbatim under
independent re-read, and the ITB-054 `A4-10 → A7-10` correction was real. The failures are
upstream of vision — wrong revision of the right sheet, and drawings treated as the primary
authority when the scope narratives were the authority all along.
