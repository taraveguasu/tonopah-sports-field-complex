LARGER THAN 1/8 INCHES IN MAXIMUM DIMENSIONS. ALL PIPING IS TO BE INSTALLED
IN AN INDIVIDUAL TRENCH, NO OTHER PIPING IS TO PLACED IN THE SAME TRENCH.
2.  ALL PIPING SHALL HAVE A MINIMUM OF 4" OF SAND AROUND THE PIPING.
3.  BACKFILL OVER IRRIGATION LINES SHALL NOT OCCUR UNTIL FULLY INSPECTED.
4.  NO LATERAL LINES SHALL BE PERMITTED IN THE SAME TRENCH AS THE MAIN LINE.  DO
NOT USE CROSS FITTINGS ON ANY PIPING. ALL PIPING MUST HAVE A MINIMUM OF 6"
CLEARANCE BETWEEN ALL PIPING EXCEPT FOR WIRE SLEEVING.
5.  WIRE CAN BE INSTALLED IN THE SAME TRENCH AS THE LATERAL AND MAIN LINES BUT
TO THE SIDE OF THE PIPE.

DRIVEWAYS, WALLS AND OTHER HARDSCAPE AND OBSTRUCTIONS.
