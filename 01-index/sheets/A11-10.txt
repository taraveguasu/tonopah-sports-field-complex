GENERAL NOTES
EXIT DOORS SHALL BE OPERABLE FROM THE INSIDE WITHOUT THE USE OF A KEY OR ANY SPECIAL KNOWLEDGE OR EFFORT. 
DOOR OPERATING DEVICES SHALL BE LEVER OPERATED, PUSH TYPE OR U-SHAPED HANDLES PER ANSI A117.1
THE BOTTOM 10" OF ALL DOORS SHALL BE PROVIDED WITH A SMOOTH SURFACE TO ALLOW THE DOOR TO BE OPENED WITH A WHEELCHAIR FOOTREST PER ANSI A117.1
EXTERIOR HOLLOW METAL DOORS AND FRAMES SHALL BE PAINTED AS NOTED ON THE EXTERIOR ELEVATIONS AND ON THE INTERIOR SIDES AS NOTED PER THE DOOR SCHEDULE.
HARDWARE: ALL HARDWARE SHALL COMPLY WITH APPLICABLE PROVISIONS OF ADA STANDARDS AND ICC A117.1.  REFER TO HARDWARE SETS FOR FINISH, DESCRIPTIONS, REQUIREMENTS 
FOR DESIGN, FUNCTION SIZE, OPERATION, AND MOUNTING LOCATIONS. 
DOOR JAMB, HEAD AND SILL DETAILS AS INDICATED REFLECT DESIGN INTENT, LOCATIONS, AND PROFILES.  DETAILS NOT SHOWN SHALL BE SIMILAR IN CHARACTER TO THOSE DETAILS. 
WHERE NOT CLEARLY DEFINED, CLARIFICATIONS SHALL BE REQUESTED BEFORE PROCEEDING WITH THAT PORTION OF THE WORK.
ALL EXTERIOR DOORS SHALL BE WEATHER STRIPPED.
ALL THRESHOLDS PROVIDED SHALL BE A MAXIMUM OF 1/2" HEIGHT ABOVE FINISH FLOOR. SEE 1/CA11-10.
ADJUST ALL DOORS NOT TO EXCEED TO THE MAXIMUM OPERATIONS EFFORT ALLOWED BY CODE.
