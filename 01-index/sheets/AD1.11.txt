THE GENERAL CONTRACTOR SHALL COORDINATE ACCESS WITH NCSD FOR THEIR 2 WEEK 
SCHEDULED SALVAGE EFFORT.
THE GENERAL CONTRACTOR SHALL VERIFY AND CONFIRM THAT ALL UTILITIES ON SITE 
ARE PROPERLY DISABLED AND/OR DISCONNECTED AND SECURELY CAPPED BEFORE 
DEMOLITION BEGINS.
FIELD VERIFY EXISTING CONDITIONS PRIOR TO THE START OF DEMOLITION OPERATIONS. 
BRING ANY DISCREPANCIES WHICH MAY SIGNIFICANTLY AFFECT DEMOLITION OR NEW 
CONSTRUCTION WORK TO THE ATTENTION OF THE ARCHITECT FOR REVIEW.
PROTECT EXISTING STRUCTURES TO REMAIN FROM DAMAGE DURING DEMOLITION. 
CONDUCT DEMOLITION TO MINIMIZE THE DEVELOPMENT AND SPREAD OF DUST.
REMOVE DEMOLITION MATERIALS FROM SITE PROMPTLY AND DISPOSE OF LEGALLY OFF 
SITE.
PROVIDE DEMOLITION WORK SHOWN ON THE DRAWINGS AND RELATED INCIDENTAL 
DEMOLITION WORK REQUIRED TO COMPLETE NEW CONSTRUCTION WORK. REFER TO 
CIVIL, PLUMBING. MECHANICAL AND ELECTRICAL FOR ADDITIONAL DEMOLITION SCOPE 
AND REQUIREMENTS.
MAINTAIN MEANS OF EGRESS AND KEEP FULLY SEPARATE FROM CONSTRUCTION AREA 
AT ALL TIMES.
CONDUCT DEMOLITION ACTIVITIES TO PREVENT INJURY TO PEOPLE AND DAMAGE TO 
ADJACENT BUILDING AREAS. ENSURE SAFE PASSAGE OF PEOPLE AROUND SELECTIVE 
DEMOLITION AREAS. IT IS ANTICIPATED THAT BUILDING 1 AND BULIDING 2 WILL BE 
OCCUPIED DURING DEMOLITION. COORDINATE WITH OWNER.
CLEAN ADJACENT STRUCTURES AND IMPROVEMENTS OF DUST, DIRT AND DEBRIS 
CAUSED BY SELECTIVE DEMOLITION AND NEW CONSTRUCTION OPERATIONS.
COMPLETED SITE DEMOLITION WITHIN THE LIMITS OF WORK SHALL BE IN A CONDITION 
THAT PREPARES THE SITE FOR NEW CONSTRUCTION THAT INCLUDES BUT IS NOT LIMITED 
TO GRADING, NEW UNDERGROUND UTILITY INSTALLATION AND ABOVE GROUND NEW 
CONSTRUCTION.
IF THE PRESENCE OF ASBESTOS IS DETERMINED PRIOR TO DEMOLITION. THE 
CONTRACTOR SHALL REVIEW THE SURVEY AND RETAIN A LEAD AND ASBESTOS 
ABATEMENT CONTRACTOR TO REMOVE ALL CONTAMINANTS PRIOR TO DEMOLITION IN 
ACCORDANCE WITH APPLICABLE REGULATIONS. A LICENSED LEAD ASBESTOS 
CONTRACTOR SHALL COORDINATE THE WORK, PROVIDE SCHEDULES AND ADMINISTER 
PAPERWORK WITH GENERAL CONTRACTOR FOR THE SURVEY AND INSPECTION REPORT. 
CONTRACTOR SHALL REPORT TO THE OWNER FOR INSTRUCTIONS IF THERE IS ANY MOLD 
DISCOVERY DURING INVESTIGATION PRIOR TO DEMOLITION.
