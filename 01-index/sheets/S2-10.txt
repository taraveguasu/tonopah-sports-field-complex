FOUNDATION PLAN NOTES:
1. CONTRACTOR TO VERIFY ALL DIMENSIONS 
PRIOR TO CONSTRUCTION.  CONTRACTOR TO 
RESOLVE ANY DISCREPANCIES WITH THE 
ENGINEER.
2. CONTRACTOR TO VERIFY LOCATION OF ALL 
DEPRESSED SLABS, INSERTS IN SLAB ON 
GRADE, ETC. WITH THE APPROVED 
ARCHITECTURAL, STRUCTURAL, MECHANICAL, 
ELECTRICAL AND PLUMBING DRAWINGS.
3. FOR CONCRETE SLAB CONTROL JOINT, SEE 
TYPICAL DETAILS AND GENERAL NOTES.
4. CF1.0, CF2.0, ETC. INDICATES CONTINUOUS 
WALL FOOTING.  SEE CONTINUOUS WALL 
FOOTING SCHEDULE.
5. WHERE FLOOR DRAINS OCCUR, PROVIDE 
MINIMUM SLOPE OF 1/8" PER FOOT FROM 
DRAIN TO ALL EDGES OF ROOM UON.  VERIFY 
PROFILE OF ALL SLAB SLOPES WITH 
ARCHITECT.
6. MJ1, MJ2, ETC. INDICATES MASONRY WALL.  
SEE SCHEDULE.  JAMBS TO EXTEND FULL 
HEIGHT OF WALL.
7. MW1, MW2, ETC. INDICATES MASONRY WALL.  
SEE SCHEDULE.  WALL TYPE SHOWN IS TO 
EXTEND FULL HEIGHT OF WALL.
8. "MCJ" INDICATES MASONRY WALL 
CONSTRUCTION JOINT.  SEE TYPICAL DETAILS 
AND GENERAL NOTES.  JOINTS SHOWN ARE TO 
EXTEND FULL HEIGHT OF WALL.
9. COORDINATE EXACT LOCATION AND DEPTH OF 
PIPING WITH MECHANICAL, ELECTRICAL, 
PLUMBING, CIVIL AND ARCHITECTURAL PLANS 
AS APPLICABLE.  
FRAMING PLAN NOTES:
1. VERIFY ALL ELEVATIONS SHOWN ON THIS 
PLAN WITH LATEST ARCHITECTURAL.
2. ROOF SLOPE AND CRICKET AREAS, SEE 
ARCHITECTURAL.
3. ROOF DECK TO BE 1 1/2" x 20 GAGE B-DECK 
(VERCO PLB-36 OR APPROVED EQUAL).  SEE 
GENERAL NOTES FOR DECK ATTACHMENT.
4. ML1, ML2 . . . INDICATES MASONRY LINTEL, SEE 
DETAIL 10/S5-00.
5. LOCATION OF MECHANICAL CHASES AND 
OTHER OPENINGS MAY NOT NECCESSARILY BE 
SHOWN ON STRUCTURAL DRAWINGS.  TRIM 
OPENINGS PER APPROPRIATE TYPICAL 
DETAILS.
6. SMALL OPENING IN ROOF, SEE DETAIL 5/S5-00 
FOR TYPICAL FRAMING.  LARGE OPENING IN 
ROOF, SEE DETAIL 4.S5-00 FOR TYPICAL 
FRAMING.  COORDINATE EXACT SIZE AND 
LOCATION OF ROOF OPENINGS WITH 
ARCHITECTURAL AND MECHANICAL 
DRAWINGS.
