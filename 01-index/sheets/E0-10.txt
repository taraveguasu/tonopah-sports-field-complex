TECHNOLOGY GENERAL NOTES
GENERAL
1. 
DO NOT SCALE DRAWINGS.  VERIFY DIMENSIONS IN FIELD PRIOR TO COMMENCEMENT OF WORK.
2. 
WHEREVER THE WORD "PROVIDE" IS USED, IT SHALL MEAN TO "PROVIDE AND INSTALL".
3. 
FINAL CONNECTIONS TO EQUIPMENT SHALL BE PER MANUFACTURER'S APPROVED WIRING DIAGRAMS, 
DETAILS AND INSTRUCTIONS.  IT SHALL BE THE CONTRACTOR'S RESPONSIBILITY TO PROVIDE MATERIALS AND 
EQUIPMENT COMPATIBLE WITH EQUIPMENT ACTUALLY SUPPLIED.
4. 
IT IS THE INTENT OF THESE DRAWINGS AND SPECIFICATIONS TO ESTABLISH A STANDARD OF QUALITY.  THE 
ENGINEER RESERVES THE RIGHT TO APPROVE METHODS AND MATERIALS NOT REFLECTED HEREIN.
5. 
CONTRACTOR SHALL REVIEW ARCHITECTURAL, STRUCTURAL, MECHANICAL AND OTHER RELATED DRAWINGS 
PRIOR TO BID.
6. 
CONTRACTOR SHALL VISIT SITE PRIOR TO BID AND VERIFY THAT CONDITIONS ARE AS INDICATED IN THE 
CONTRACT DOCUMENTS.  CONTRACTOR SHALL INCLUDE IN HIS BID, ANY COSTS REQUIRED TO MAKE HIS 
WORK MEET THE CONTRACT SCOPE UTILIZING EXISTING CONDITIONS.
7. 
WORK SHALL BE PERFORMED IN A WORKMANLIKE MANNER TO THE SATISFACTION OF THE ARCHITECT.
8. 
WORK, MATERIALS AND EQUIPMENT SHALL CONFORM TO THE LATEST EDITIONS OF LOCAL, STATE AND 
NATIONAL CODES AND ORDINANCES.
9. 
PROVIDE PERMITS AND INSPECTIONS REQUIRED.
10. 
VERIFY SPECIFIC LOCATION OF EQUIPMENT TO BE FURNISHED BY OTHERS PRIOR TO ROUGH-IN.
11. 
SYSTEM OUTAGES SHALL BE PERMITTED ONLY AT TIMES APPROVED BY OWNER, IN WRITING.  WORK WHICH 
COULD RESULT IN AN ACCIDENTAL OUTAGE (BEYOND BRANCH CIRCUITS) SHALL BE PERFORMED WITH THE 
OWNER'S MAINTENANCE PERSONNEL ADVISED OF SUCH WORK.
12. 
CONTRACTOR'S FAILURE TO ORDER OR RELEASE ORDER FOR MATERIALS AND/OR EQUIPMENT WILL NOT BE 
ACCEPTED AS A REASON TO SUBSTITUTE ALTERNATE MATERIALS, EQUIPMENT OR INSTALLATION METHODS.
13. 
EXAMINATION OF BIDDING DOCUMENTS.
• 
EACH BIDDER SHALL EXAMINE THE BIDDING DOCUMENTS CAREFULLY AND, NOT LATER THAN SEVEN 
DAYS PRIOR TO THE DATE OF RECEIPT OF BIDS, SHALL MAKE WRITTEN REQUEST TO THE 
ARCHITECT/ENGINEER FOR INTERPRETATION OR CORRECTION OF ANY DISCREPANCIES, AMBIGUITY, 
INCONSISTENCY OR ERROR THEREIN WHICH HE MAY DISCOVER.  ANY INTERPRETATION OR 
CORRECTION WILL BE ISSUED AS AN ADDENDUM BY THE ARCHITECT.  ONLY A WRITTEN 
INTERPRETATION OR CORRECTION BY ADDENDUM SHALL BE BINDING.  NO BIDDER SHALL RELY UPON 
INTERPRETATIONS OR CORRECTIONS GIVEN BY ANY OTHER METHOD.  IF DISCREPANCIES, AMBIGUITY, 
INCONSISTENCY OR ERROR ARE NOT COVERED BY ADDENDUM OR WRITTEN DIRECTIVE, CONTRACTOR 
SHALL INCLUDE IN HIS BID, LABOR, MATERIALS AND METHODS OF CONSTRUCTION RESULTING IN 
HIGHER COST.  AFTER AWARD OF CONTRACT, NO ALLOWANCE OR EXTRA COMPENSATION WILL BE 
MADE IN BEHALF OF THE CONTRACTOR DUE TO HIS FAILURE TO MAKE THE WRITTEN REQUESTS AS 
DESCRIBED ABOVE.
• 
THE PERSON SUBMITTING THE REQUEST WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR ITS PROMPT DELIVERY.  FAILURE 
TO SO REQUEST CLARIFICATION OF ANY INADEQUACY, OMISSION OR CONFLICT WILL NOT RELIEVE THE 
CONTRACTOR OF RESPONSIBILITY.  THE SIGNING OF THE CONTRACT WILL BE CONSIDERED AS 
IMPLICITLY DENOTING THAT THE CONTRACTOR HAS A THOROUGH COMPREHENSION OF THE FULL 
INTENT AND SCOPE OF THE WORKING DRAWINGS AND SPECIFICATIONS.
14. 
PROPOSED SUBSTITUTIONS OF TECHNOLOGY EQUIPMENT OR REQUEST FOR "OR EQUIVALENT" OR 
"APPROVED EQUIVALENT" LISTING SHALL BE SUBMITTED TO ARCHITECT NOT LESS THAN TEN (10) WORKING 
DAYS PRIOR TO BID.  REFER TO SPECIFICATIONS FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION.
DEMOLITION
15. 
ALL ABANDONED ELECTRICAL CONDUCTORS, AND RACEWAYS SHALL BE REMOVED BACK TO THE SOURCE.
16. 
ALL EXPOSED WIRING THAT IS NOT IN USE SHALL BE REMOVED BACK TO SOURCE (ABOVE OR BELOW GRADE). 
IF EXISTING EXPOSED WIRING IS IN USE, IT SHALL BE INSTALLED IN CONDUIT.
17. 
EXISTING TECHNOLOGY EQUIPMENT, DEVICES, ETC., BEING REMOVED AND NOT REINSTALLED SHALL BE 
OFFERED TO THE OWNER, BEFORE IT IS DISCARDED.
UNDERGROUND
18. 
VERIFY SPECIFIC LOCATIONS OF EXISTING AND NEW UNDERGROUND UTILITIES, PIPING AND RACEWAY 
SYSTEMS PRIOR TO TRENCHING.  PROVIDE NECESSARY TRENCHING, BACKFILL, EXCAVATION, SUPPORTS, 
PULLBOXES, SAWCUTTING AND PATCHING, CONCRETE/PAVING, ETC., REQUIRED.  BACKFILL TRENCHES TO 90% 
COMPACTION AND PATCH TO MATCH EXISTING.  CONTRACTOR SHALL OBTAIN AND VERIFY SPECIFIC UTILITY 
COMPANY DRAWINGS AND REQUIREMENTS.
19. 
CABLING FOR TECHNOLOGY SYSTEMS SHALL NOT BE SPLICED IN EXTERIOR PULLBOXES OR MANHOLES. SEAL 
ENDS OF CONDUITS AND DUCTS WITH "DUCTSEAL" OR APPROVED EQUIVALENT.
RACEWAYS AND JUNCTION BOXES
20. 
THE MINIMUM SIZE DIAMETER FOR DATA AND SURVEILLANCE CAMERAS SHALL BE 1” MINIMUM DIAMETER.
21. 
BOXES FOR WIRING AND DEVICES SHALL BE MINIMUM 4" SQUARE.
22. 
JUNCTION BOXES THAT CONNECT TO CONDUITS GREATER THAN 1 1/4" SHALL BE SIZED PER TIA-569 (INDOOR) 
OR TIA-758 (OUTDOOR).
23. 
PULLBOXES, CABINETS, ETC., MOUNTED ON THE EXTERIOR AT GRADE LEVEL, SHALL BE WEATHERPROOF TYPE 
WITH HINGED LOCKABLE COVERS SECURED WITH TAMPER-PROOF SCREWS.
24. 
ALL WIRING SHALL BE INSTALLED IN LISTED METALLIC RACEWAYS WITH STEEL COMPRESSION CONNECTORS. 
(DIECAST OR SET-SCREW ARE NOT ALLOWED).  RACEWAYS IN SLAB-ON-GRADE OR BELOW GRADE SHALL BE 
SCHEDULE 40 PVC ENCASED IN CONCRETE.  PVC-COATED RIGID STEEL FACTORY ELBOWS SHALL BE 
PROVIDED FOR BENDS IN PVC RUNS LONGER THAN 100 FEET. ELECTRICAL RUNS SHALL NOT EXCEED 180 
DEGREES OF BENDS (TO INCLUDE KICKS, OFFSETS, SWEEPS).  BETWEEN JUNCTION BOXES.
25. 
EXPOSED WIRING TRAYS OR EXPOSED CONDUITS WITHIN THE BUILDING ARE EXPRESSLY FORBIDDEN 
WITHOUT THE PRIOR APPROVAL AND CONSENT OF ARCHITECT OR ENGINEER.  EMT CONDUIT AND FITTINGS 
SHALL BE USED FOR CONDUIT INSIDE OF WALLS OR ABOVE THE CEILING.
26. 
RIGID STEEL CONDUIT AND FITTINGS SHALL BE USED FOR ALL EXTERIOR CONDUITS EXPOSED TO PHYSICAL 
DAMAGE.  ALL CONDUITS SHALL BE INSTALLED AND RIGIDLY SUPPORTED USING APPROVED CONDUIT 
HANGERS, UNI-STRUT, OR ALL THREAD AS REQUIRED.
27. 
ALL EMPTY RACEWAY SYSTEMS SHALL HAVE A PULL STRING OR EQUIVALENT AND SHALL BE IDENTIFIED AT ALL 
JUNCTION, PULL AND TERMINATION POINTS, USING PERMANENT METALLIC TAGS.  TAG SHALL INDICATE 
INTENDED USE OF CONDUIT, ORIGINATION AND TERMINATION POINTS OF EACH INDIVIDUAL CONDUIT. 
28. 
CONDUITS PENETRATING THRU ROOF SHALL HAVE ROOF FLASHING WITH CAULK TYPE COUNTER FLASHING 
SLEEVE.  INSTALLATION SHALL BE WATERTIGHT.
29. 
DO NOT INSTALL JUNCTION BOXES BACK-TO-BACK IN WALLS.  PROVIDE A MINIMUM 6 INCH (150MM) 
SEPARATION EXCEPT PROVIDE A MINIMUM 24 INCH (600MM) SEPARATION IN ACOUSTIC AND FIRE RATED 
WALLS.  WHERE A 24 INCH SEPARATION IN ACOUSTIC AND/OR FIRE RATED WALLS IS NOT POSSIBLE, PROVIDE 
LISTED SOUND PUTTY PAD OR FIRE RATED PUTTY PAD WHERE APPLICABLE.  THE CONTRACTOR SHALL BE 
REQUIRED TO COORDINATE THE JUNCTION BOX SIZE AND THE DERATING OF CONDUCTORS WHEN USING AN 
INSERT TYPE PUTTY PAD WITHIN THE INTERIOR OF THE JUNCTION BOX.  THESE MATERIALS SHALL BE 
SUBMITTED TO THE ARCHITECT AND THE ENGINEER FOR APPROVAL.
30. 
FINAL CONNECTIONS TO VIBRATING EQUIPMENT SHALL BE WITH FLEX (LIQUIDTIGHT FOR EXTERIOR 
APPLICATIONS) AND APPROVED FITTINGS.  DO NOT SECURE CONDUITS, DISCONNECTS OR DEVICES TO 
DUCTWORK OR MECHANICAL EQUIPMENT.
31. 
PLASTIC BUSHINGS ARE REQUIRED ON ALL CONNECTORS, PRIOR TO PULLING WIRE, 1” AND SMALLER; 1-1/4" 
AND LARGER SHALL HAVE GROUND BUSHINGS. ALL SLEEVES SHALL HAVE PLASTIC BUSHINGS INSTALLED 
PRIOR TO PULLING CABLE THROUGH THEM.
LABELING
32. 
ALL CABLING SHALL BE LABELED AT THE FACEPLATE AND TERMINATION FIELD IN THE IT ROOM.  ALL CABLES 
SHALL HAVE SIMILAR LABEL 6" BEHIND TERMINATIONS - PRINTED, NOT WRITTEN.  ALL SPEAKERS SHALL HAVE A 
LABEL ON THE EXPOSED SPEAKER FRAME AND FACEPLATES LABELED FOR OTHER LOCATIONS.  LABELS TO 
IDENTIFY ROOM OF TERMINATION AND ORIGIN. NO TWO LABELS SHALL BE THE SAME.  DEPENDING ON TYPE 
(WIRELESS, DATA OUTLET, IP SPEAKER, CAMERA), LABEL SHALL ALSO INCLUDE AN ABBREVIATION OF THIS 
INFORMATION FOR EASE OF TRACING AND TROUBLESHOOTING.  LABELING SHALL COMPLY WITH TIA-606-D.
RECEPTACLES AND DEVICES
33. 
RECEPTACLES WHICH ARE SHOWN WALL MOUNTED ON THE ELECTRICAL DRAWINGS ON WALLS WHICH, ON 
THE ARCHITECTURAL DRAWINGS AND ELEVATIONS ARE SHOWN AS GLASS OR PARTITIONS, SHALL BE FLUSH 
FLOOR DUPLEX RECEPTACLES MOUNTED ADJACENT TO BASE OR WALLS.
34. 
RECEPTACLES AT COUNTER SHALL BE MOUNTED WITH THEIR LONG AXIS HORIZONTAL AT +46" UNLESS NOTED.
35. 
THE CONTRACTOR SHALL COORDINATE THE SPECIFIC LOCATION, MOUNTING HEIGHT, ROTATION, TYPE, 
COLOR, ETC. OF ALL DEVICES PRIOR TO INSTALLATION.
TECHNOLOGY SYSTEMS
36. 
THE MINIMUM SIZE DIAMETER FOR ALL DATA AND SURVEILLANCE CAMERA CONDUITS IS 1” DIAMETER .
37. 
ALL TECHNOLOGY WIRING CONCEALED IN WALLS SHALL BE IN CONDUIT AND IDENTIFIED (EG: TELEPHONE, 
SECURITY, PAGING, EMS, ETC.).
38. 
TECHNOLOGY CONDUIT RUNS ON OR IN BUILDINGS SHALL NOT EXCEED 180 DEGREES OF BEND OR 100' IN 
LENGTH BETWEEN JUNCTION BOXES.
39. 
DATA RUNS SHALL NOT CHANGE DIRECTIONS IN A JUNCTION BOX. ALL DIRECTIONAL CHANGES SHALL BE MADE 
IN THE RACEWAY WITH PROPER RADIUS BEND.
40. 
TELEPHONE INSTALLATION SHALL NOT HAVE ANY COMPONENTS IN COMMON WITH THE POWER OR LIGHTING 
INSTALLATION.
41. 
TELEPHONE BACKBOARDS SHALL BE 3/4" A/C RATED FIRE RATED PLYWOOD WITH C SIDE AGAINST 
WALL. BOARD SHALL BE PAINTED WITH (TWO COATS) WHITE FIRE RESISTANT PAINT ON ALL SIDES.
42. 
BOXES FOR TELEPHONE, T.V., COMPUTER, DEVICES, ETC., SHALL BE MINIMUM 4" SQUARE.
43. 
ALL DATA CABLES SHALL BE CAT6. BLUE FOR DATA.
44. 
TELEPHONE BOARDS SHALL BE 3/4" A-C GRADE OR BETTER, VOID-FREE PLYWOOD, (8FT) HIGH WITH THE "C" 
SURFACE FACING THE WALL. THE PLYWOOD SHALL BE TREATED ON ALL SIDES WITH AT LEAST TWO (2) COATS 
OF FLAT WHITE, FIRE-RESISTANT PAINT. SECURELY FASTEN THE PLYWOOD TO WALL-FRAMING MEMBERS.
SUBMITTALS
45. 
PROVIDE SHOP DRAWING SUBMITTAL DATA AT ONE TIME, IN ELECTRICAL PDF FORMAT, INDEXED IN A NEAT 
AND ORDERLY MANNER.  PARTIAL SUBMITTALS WILL NOT BE ACCEPTED.
46. 
PROVIDE RECORD DRAWINGS TO ENGINEER.  DRAWINGS SHALL INCLUDE ALL ADDENDUM ITEMS, CHANGE 
ORDERS, ALTERATIONS, REROUTINGS, ETC. DRAWINGS SHALL BE PROFESSIONALLY RED-LINED, 
ELECTRONICALLY OR UPDATED IN CAD/REVIT BY CONTRACTOR.
WARRANTY / QUALITY CONTROL
47. 
ELECTRICAL SYSTEMS SHALL BE COMPLETE, OPERABLE AND READY FOR CONTINUOUS OPERATION AT 
COMPLETION OF PROJECT. 
48. 
CONTRACTOR SHALL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR REPLACING EQUIPMENT WHICH IS DAMAGED  DUE TO INCORRECT 
FIELD WIRING PROVIDED UNDER THIS SECTION OR FACTORY WIRING IN EQUIPMENT PROVIDED UNDER THIS 
SECTION.
49. 
GUARANTEE THE INSTALLATION AGAINST DEFECTS IN MATERIALS AND WORKMANSHIP WHICH MAY OCCUR 
UNDER NORMAL USAGE FOR A PERIOD OF ONE YEAR AFTER OWNER'S ACCEPTANCE.  DEFECTS SHALL BE 
PROMPTLY REMEDIED WITHOUT COST TO THE OWNER.  
TECHNOLOGY
50. 
TECHNOLOGY SYSTEMS SHALL BE TESTED FOR PROPER OPERATION.  IF TESTS SHOW THAT WORK IS 
DEFECTIVE, CONTRACTOR SHALL MAKE CORRECTIONS NECESSARY AT NO COST TO OWNER.
51. 
CONTRACTOR SHALL USE A CAT6 OR CAT6A CERTIFYING TESTER FOR CLASS E COPPER CABLING.   TESTS 
SHALL BE MADE USING THE CORRECT NVP AND MANUFACTURER/MODEL NUMBER OF CABLING FOR THE TEST.  
DATA OUTLETS AND WIRELESS OUTLETS SHALL BE CERTIFIED USING A PERMANENT LINK TEST FROM PATCH 
PANEL TO JACK AT DEVICE/WORKSTATION.  SECURITY CAMERA AND IP POE SPEAKER CABLING SHALL USE A 
MPTL (MODULAR PLUG TERMINATED LINK) TEST FROM THE PATCH PANEL TO 8-PIN, 8-POSITION MODULAR 
PLUG.  SUBMIT ALL TEST REPORTS IN PDF FORMAT WITH SUMMARY REPORT IN FRONT.  DO NOT SUBMIT ANY 
FAILURES OR CONDITIONAL PASSES.  SUBMIT AT LEAST TWO WEEKS PRIOR TO ANY SCHEDULED TURNOVER 
OR BRINGING UP SWITCHES AND DEVICES OVER THE CABLING.  TESTER SHALL BE WITHIN 11 MONTHS OF LAST 
CALIBRATION BY MANUFACTURER AND DATE REFLECTED IN TEST REPORTS. 
52. 
ALL FIBER OPTIC CABLE SHALL BE TESTED USING A POWER METER (OPTICAL LOSS TEST SET) AND OTDR 
(OPTICAL TIME DOMAIN REFLECTOMETER).  PERFORM A TIER I AND TIER II TEST OF FIBER OPTIC CABLE AT 
850NM AND 1300NM. USE ANENCIRCLED FLUX LAUNCH CONTROLLER TO AVOID MISTAKES USING A MANDREL.  
DO NOT SUBMIT TESTS WITH ANY FAILURES.  USE OM4 LC FIBER OPTIC CONNECTORS AND TERMINATE ALL 
STRANDS ON BOTH ENDS.  USE THE ONE-CORD REFERENCE METHOD (ALSO REFERRED TO AS THE ONE-
JUMPER REFERENCE METHOD).
53. 
ADDITIONAL CONTRACTOR FURNISHED AND INSTALLED MATERIALS.
54. 
CONTRACTOR SHALL FURNISH ALL CATEGORY COPPER CABLE PATCH CORDS AND FIBER OPTIC PATCH CORDS 
TO CONNECT BETWEEN THE NETWORK SWITCH AND PATCH PANEL.  RATING OF PATCH CABLE SHALL MATCH 
CABLING.  ALL FIBER OPTIC PATCH CORDS SHALL BE LC-LC DUPLEX.
CLOCK-INTERCOM SYSTEM
55. 
OWNER REQUIRES ALL IP POE SPEAKERS TO BE OPERATIONAL WITH RAW MULTICAST BROADCASTS, WITHOUT 
THE NEED FOR A SIP SERVER.  SPEAKER SHOULD BE FUNCITONAL IN BOTH SCENARIOS.  RECOMMEND ALGO 
OR ATLAS SOUND IED IP POE SPEAKERS.  CONTRACTOR SHALL PURCHASE AND PROVIDE A SAMPLE SPEAKER 
TO OWNER TO VERIFY COMPLIANCE - THIS SHALL OCCUR IMMEDIATELY UPON CONDITIONAL APPROVAL OF 
SUBMITTAL.  PRODUCT WILL NOT BE CONSIDERED APPROVED UNTIL AFTER OWNER HAS TESTED PRODUCT.  
CONTRACTOR SHALL INCLUDE ALL HOUSINGS AND BRACKETS REQUIRED FOR INSTALLATION BASED ON 
LOCATION.
A. ALGO INDOOR SURFACE MOUNT POE WALL SPEAKER #8189
B. ALGO IP POE CEILING SPEAKER #8188
C. ALGO OUTDOOR WP POE+ HORN #8197
D. ATLAS IED INDOOR SURFACE MOUNT POE WALL SPEAKER # IP-8SM WITH ANGLED WALL MOUNT 
SURFACE ENCLOSURE #IP-SEA-S
E. ATLAS IED CEILING SPEAKER #IP-8SM WITH CEILING TILE #IP-STBE AND BACKBOX #IP-SEST-S.
F. ATLAS IED OUTDOOR WP IP POE+ HORN #IP-APX.
SECURITY CAMERA SYSTEM
56. 
CONTRACTOR SHALL COORDINATE WITH OWNER FOR SURVIELLANCE CAMERAS AND MOUNTS.  INSTALL 
OWNER PROVIDED CAMERAS. THE SECURTY CAMERA SYSTEM SHALL HAVE ITS OWN FIBER OPTIC CABLE 
STRANDS AND SWITCH. DO NOT CONNECT OTHER SYSTEMS TO THE SECURITY DESIGNATED SWITCH.
