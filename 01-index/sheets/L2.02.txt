IRRIGATION DEMOLITION NOTES
1.
SEE CONSTRUCTION NOTES ON SHEET L1.00.
2.
VERIFY EXISTING IRRIGATION COMPONENTS' LOCATIONS PRIOR TO START OF WORK. THE EXISTING IRRIGATION
INFORMATION USED FOR THESE PLANS IS BASED ON LIMITED INFORMATION. NO AS-BUILT OR RECORD DRAWINGS
WERE AVAILABLE. VERIFY THAT THE EXISTING MAINLINE FOR THE NEW IRRIGATION POINT OF CONNECTION IS
FUNCTIONING PROPERLY AND IN GOOD CONDITION. CONTACT OWNER'S REPRESENTATIVE WITH ANY DEFECTS OR
CONCERNS.
3.
CONTRACTOR TO REMOVE EXISTING GATE VALVES, QUICK COUPLING ASSEMBLIES AND MAINLINE WITHIN THE
FOOTBALL FIELD AREA, AS NOTED ON PLANS, FOR REPLACEMENT WITH NEW VALVES AND MAINLINE.  IRRIGATION
LINES AND COMPONENTS THAT ARE NECESSARY FOR THE THE CURRENT AND CONTINUOUS IRRIGATION OF THE
EXISTING CAMPUS' LANDSCAPE SHALL BE PROTECTED IN PLACE.  SEE IRRIGATION PLAN FOR ADDITIONAL
INFORMATION.
4.
COORDINATE INSTALLATION OF IRRIGATION WITH DRAINAGE SYSTEM AND THE DRAINAGE COMPONENTS'
LOCATIONS. CONTRACTOR SHALL PROTECT IN PLACE THE DRAINAGE SYSTEM. DAMAGES TO THE PERMANENT
DRAINAGE SYSTEM, DUE TO CONSTRUCTION, SHALL BE REPAIRED AT THE CONTRACTOR'S EXPENSE.
5.
CONTRACTOR TO MAINTAIN IRRIGATION TO EXISTING PLANT MATERIAL ON CAMPUS DURING DEMOLITION AND
CONSTRUCTION. HAND WATERING AND/OR TEMPORARY IRRIGATION MAY BE NECESSARY. IF TEMPORARY OR HAND
WATERING IS NECESSARY, CONTRACTOR SHALL SUBMIT METHOD OF IRRIGATION AND WATERING SCHEDULE TO
OWNER'S REPRESENTATIVE FOR WRITTEN APPROVAL, PRIOR TO BEGINNING OF CONSTRUCTION.
6.
CONTRACTOR TO PROTECT AND PRESERVE THE EXISTING TRACK SYSTEM (SURFACE, SUBBASE, SUBGRADE AND
STRIPING). CONTRACTOR TO RETURN ANY DAMAGE TO THE TRACK SYSTEM BACK TO ITS ORIGINAL CONDITION, AT
CONTRACTOR'S EXPENSE. METHODS FOR TRACK REPAIR SHALL BE APPROVED BY OWNER'S REPRESENTATIVE.
7.
CONTRACTOR TO REPAIR ANY EXISTING IRRIGATION TO REMAIN THAT IS DAMAGED DUE TO CONSTRUCTION AND
TO REPLACE ANY PLANT MATERIAL DAMAGED DURING CONSTRUCTION AT CONTRACTOR'S EXPENSE.
8.
DISPOSE OF ALL DEMOLISHED MATERIAL OFF SITE IN A LEGAL MANNER.
9.
CONTRACTOR TO USE CAUTION WHEN REMOVING EXISTING TURF AND IRRIGATION.
10.
CALL BEFORE YOU DIG AND CALL BEFORE YOU OVERHEAD. AVOID ALL EXISTING OBSTRUCTIONS AND UTILITIES.
11. BRING ANY DISCREPANCIES IMMEDIATELY TO THE ATTENTION OF THE OWNER'S REPRESENTATIVE FOR
RESOLUTION.
IRRIGATION NOTES
1.
CONTRACTOR TO FIELD VERIFY EXISTING CONDITIONS AND VERIFY AND LOCATE EXISTING IRRIGATION COMPONENTS
PRIOR TO BEGINNING WORK. CONTRACTOR TO VERIFY THAT THE  EXISTING IRRIGATION COMPONENTS ARE FUNCTIONING
PROPERLY INCLUDING THE METER, BACKFLOW PREVENTER AND MAINLINES TO THE FOOTBALL FIELD AREA. VERIFY THAT
THE IRRIGATION MAINLINE TO BE USED AS THE POINT OF CONNECTION FOR THE REPLACED/NEW FOOTBALL
QUICK-COUPLING IRRIGATION AND ITS RELATED WATER SUPPLY LINES ARE IN GOOD, WORKING CONDITION.
2.
CONTRACTOR TO PROTECT AND PRESERVE EXISTING IRRIGATION COMPONENTS NECESSARY FOR THE NEW FOOTBALL
FIELD QUICK-COUPLING IRRIGATION AND THOSE NECESSARY FOR CONTINUOUS IRRIGATION OF THE EXISTING CAMPUS
AND ITS LANDSCAPE.  CONTRACTOR TO REPLACE IRRIGATION EQUIPMENT DAMAGED DURING DEMOLITION AND/OR
CONSTRUCTION WITH EQUIPMENT OF THE SAME MANUFACTURER, MODEL, VARIETY AND SIZE AT THE CONTRACTOR'S
EXPENSE.
3.
INSTALL QUICK COUPLER ASSEMBLIES OUTSIDE OF THE FIELD AREA OF PLAY. CONTRACTOR SHALL MARK OR IDENTIFY
LOCATION OF QUICK COUPLING ASSEMBLIES IN RELATIONSHIP TO PLAY AREAS, FOR APPROVAL BY OWNER'S
REPRESENTATIVE, PRIOR TO IRRIGATION INSTALLATION.
4.
INSTALL GATE VALVE(S) OUTSIDE OF THE FIELD AREA OF PLAY AND STRIPING WHEN POSSIBLE. CONTRACTOR SHALL MARK
OR IDENTIFY LOCATION OF GATE VALVE, IN RELATIONSHIP TO PLAY AREAS, FOR APPROVAL BY OWNER'S
REPRESENTATIVE, PRIOR TO IRRIGATION INSTALLATION.
5.
INSTALL NEW VALVE BOXES FOR ALL VALVE AND ALL VALVE ASSEMBLIES. INSTALL SYNTHETIC TURF ON TOP OF VALVE
BOXES IN THE SYNTHETIC TURF AREAS, PER MANUFACTURER'S RECOMMENDATIONS. TOP OF TURF ON VALVE BOXES
MUST BE FLUSH WITH TOP OF ADJACENT SYNTHETIC TURF.
6.
INSTALL MAINLINE ADJACENT AND PARALLEL TO CURB WHERE POSSIBLE. PROVIDE SEPARATION FROM DRAINAGE PIPE,
THAT ALLOWS FOR MAINTENANCE AND ACCESS TO BOTH DRAIN PIPE, IRRIGATION MAINLINE AND IRRIGATION
COMPONENTS.
7.
INSTALL NEW SLEEVES IF NECESSARY WHERE IRRIGATION PIPE IS BENEATH CONCRETE, CURB, WALLS OR OTHER
HARDSCAPES. SEE IRRIGATION SLEEVE SECTION DETAIL ON SHEET L3.01.
8.
CONTRACTOR TO PROTECT AND PRESERVE THE EXISTING TRACK SYSTEM (SURFACE, SUBBASE, SUBGRADE AND
STRIPING). CONTRACTOR TO RETURN ANY DAMAGE TO THE TRACK SYSTEM BACK TO ITS ORIGINAL CONDITION, AT
CONTRACTOR'S EXPENSE. METHODS FOR TRACK REPAIR SHALL BE APPROVED BY OWNER'S REPRESENTATIVE.
9.
PROVIDE SEPARATION BETWEEN WATER LINES AND OTHER UTILITIES AS REQUIRED PER LOCAL CODES.
10.
CONTRACTOR TO USE CAUTION WHEN INSTALLING IRRIGATION. CALL BEFORE YOU DIG AND CALL BEFORE YOU OVERHEAD.
PROTECT THE DRAINAGE SYSTEM BENEATH EXISTING TURF SYSTEM.
11.
AVOID ALL EXISTING OBSTRUCTIONS AND UTILITIES. PROTECT AND PRESERVE IN PLACE.
12. BRING ANY DISCREPANCIES IMMEDIATELY TO THE ATTENTION OF THE OWNER'S REPRESENTATIVE FOR IMMEDIATE
RESOLUTION.
