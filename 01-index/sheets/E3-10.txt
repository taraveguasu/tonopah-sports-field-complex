SHEET NOTES:
1
CONTRACTOR SHALL INSTALL RELOCATED (BIAMP) COMMUNITY R2
SPEAKER AT 30'-0" AFG ON POLE.  COORDINATE BEFORE POLE IS
PURCHASED AND INSTALLED.  CONTRACTOR SHALL INSTALL 1"
FLEXIBLE METAL CONDUIT BETWEEN HANDHOLE AT 2'-0" AND ONE
AT 30'-0".  HOLES FOR FACEPLATES MUST BE MADE DURING
MANUFACTURE OF POLES.  FLEX SHOULD BE INSTALLED AND
SECURED BEFORE THE POLE GOES VERTICAL.  SPEAKERS SHALL
FACE FIELD, AND TILTED DOWNWARD - SOUND MUST COVER FIELD
AND REACH SEATING ON OPPOSITE SIDE OF FIELD.  LIVE TEST IN
FIELD WHEN CALIBRATING AMPLIFIER AND ADJUSTING ANGLE OF
INSTALLATION.
2
FURNISH AND INSTALL THE FOLLOWING LOUDSPEAKER MOUNTING
COMPONENTS ON POLE FROM ADAPTIVE TECHNOLOGIES GROUP:
STANDARD OUTDOOR POLE MOUNT PART# PM-24-6-UP-G
GALVANIZED SAFETY ANCHOR PART# PM-SAFETY-6DOWN
BANDING KIT FOR POLES/COLUMNS PART# PM-SB-BAND-90
ALSO PURCHASE AND INSTALL APPROPRIATE SAFETY CABLES
FROM MANUFACTURER, DETERMINE TYPE OF ENDS REQUIRED
PRIOR TO ORDERING.
3
FOLLOW MANUFACTURER RECOMMENDATIONS FOR PROPER POLE
INSTALLATION.
4
FURNISH AND INSTALL IN-GRADE PULL BOX, USE 17X30 BOX WITH
APPROPRIATE COVER BASED ON LOADS WHICH WILL PASS OVER
IT.  PASS SPEAKER CABLE THROUGH BOX PRIOR TO SWEEPING UP
UNDER AND INTO POLE.
5
FURNISH AND INSTALL 14-2 OUTDOOR RATED SPEAKER CABLE
FROM POLE MOUNTED LOUDSPEAKER TO AV CABINET INSIDE
PRESS BOX.  USE WEATHERPROOF COMPRESSION CONNECTORS
ON ALL OUTDOOR CONNECTIONS.  INSTALL IN 1-1/4" C FROM PRESS
BOX AV CABINET TO IN-GROUND PULL BOX NEXT TO POLES.
INSTALL 1" CONDUIT FROM IN-GROUND PULL BOX TO EACH POLE
LOUDSPEAKER.
6
CONTRACTOR SHALL INSTALL RELOCATED (BIAMP) COMMUNITY R2
SPEAKER ON TOP OF PRESS BOX ROOF.
7
FURNISH AND INSTALL 16-2 OUTDOOR RATED SPEAKER CABLE
FROM RELOCATED ENCLOSURE IN PRESS BOX TO SPEAKERS ON
ROOF.  COORDINATE WITH ARCHITECT FOR APPROVED ENTRY
POINT.  DO NOT MAKE ROOF PENETRATION WITHOUT A/E
APPROVAL.  PLAN TO ROUTE CABLE AND GRC RIGID STEEL
CONDUIT DOWN SIDE AND PENETRATE JUST BELOW CEILING.
TRANSITION TO EMT INDOORS AND RUN ALONG CEILING OVER TO
AV CABINET. USE WEATHERPROOF COMPRESSION CONNECTORS
ON ALL OUTDOOR CONNECTIONS.
8
SPEAKER POWER SHALL BE CALIBRATED AND ADJUSTED TO WORK
IN CONJUNCTION WITH OTHER SPEAKERS ON POLES. ADJUST
ACCORDINGLY SO THERE IS NO ECHO AS A RESULT OF SPEAKERS
AT DIFFERENT DISTANCES. LIVE TEST IN FIELD WHEN CALIBRATING
AMPLIFIER POWER.
9
CONTRACTOR SHALL FURNISH AND INSTALL SIEMENS RUGGEDIZED
10 PORT POE SWITCH (PART# RST916P) WITH OM4 850 NM LC SFP
AT +56" AFF.  INSTALL DIN RAIL TO WALL AND MOUNT SWITCH TO IT.
10 INSTALL 4SQ JBOX AT +18" AFF IN WALL WITH SINGLE GANG MUD
RING.  USE 1" CONDUIT TO ROUTE OVER TO NETWORK SWITCH
LOCATION.
11 CONTRACTOR SHALL RELOCATE EXISTING AV RACK TO THIS
POSITION.
12 FURNISH AND INSTALL CONDUIT UP SUPPORT COLUMN TO SLEEVE
UP INTO PRESS BOX NEAR AV RACK.  SLEEVE UP AGAINST WALL,
NOT UNDER RACK. USE RIGID STEEL CONDUIT WHEN EXPOSED
OUTSIDE.  INSTALL PLASTIC BUSHINGS ON SLEEVES ENTERING
PRESS BOX.
