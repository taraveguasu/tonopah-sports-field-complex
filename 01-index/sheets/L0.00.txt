LANDSCAPE DEMOLITION NOTES
1.
THE CONTRACTOR IS RESPONSIBLE TO VERIFY EXISTING CONDITIONS PRIOR TO BID AND PRIOR  TO THE START OF PROJECT. THIS IS A PREVIOUSLY DEVELOPED SITE. PLANS ARE
BASED ON LIMITED INFORMATION AND ON THE EXISTING CONDITIONS AT THE TIME OF DESIGN.
2.
CONTRACTOR SHALL PHOTOGRAPHICALLY DOCUMENT EXISTING CONDITIONS PRIOR TO THE START OF ANY WORK PER SPECIFICATION SECTION 01 32 33. IMAGES SHALL INCLUDE
THE EXISTING CONDITIONS THAT ARE OUTSIDE THE LIMIT OF WORK THAT MAY BE AFFECTED BY THE SCOPE OF THE WORK.
3.
REVIEW ALL PLANS PRIOR TO DEMOLITION WORK, INCLUDING CIVIL ENGINEERING PLANS. COORDINATE DEMOLITION WITH EXISTING CONDITIONS. SEE IRRIGATION PLANS FOR
ADDITIONAL IRRIGATION INFORMATION. DISPOSE OF ITEMS INDICATED TO BE REMOVED IN A LAWFUL MANNER OFF-SITE.
4.
CONTRACTOR SHALL SUBMIT PLANS SHOWING STAGING, LAYOUT AND CONSTRUCTION LAYOUT AREAS FOR APPROVAL BY A/E PRIOR TO START OF WORK. PLANS SHALL INCLUDE
TEMPORARY CONSTRUCTION FENCING INFORMATION, CONSTRUCTION TRUCKING ACCESS ROUTE(S), SCHOOL START TIME, SCHOOL END TIME AND NO CONSTRUCTION TRUCK TIMES
BASED ON SCHOOL SCHEDULE INCLUDING SCHOOL START AND STOP TIMES. PLANS SHALL ALSO INCLUDE SECURE FENCING AS NECESSARY. ENSURE CONSTRUCTION ACTIVITIES DO
NOT OBSTRUCT SCHOOL BUS TRAFFIC, PUBLIC SIDEWALKS, FIRE LANES, AND STUDENT  TRAFFIC CORRIDORS. PLANS SHALL BE APPROVED BY A/E PRIOR TO START OF WORK.
5.
CONTRACTOR TO WALK THE SITE WITH THE A/E AND/OR NCSD/OWNER'S REPRESENTATIVE, PRIOR TO THE START OF WORK, TO VERIFY THAT ALL ATHLETIC AND SCHOOL EQUIPMENT
THAT THE SCHOOL WANTS TO KEEP HAS BEEN REMOVED FROM THE LIMITS OF CONSTRUCTION, THE LAYOUT AREAS AND CONSTRUCTION ACCESS AREAS. EQUIPMENT SHOULD HAVE
BEEN REMOVED PRIOR TO THE CONTRACTOR'S START OF WORK DATE. SCHOOL EQUIPMENT CAN INCLUDE STORAGE CONTAINERS, DISCUS CAGES, TRAINING EQUIPMENT, SOCCER
GOALS, SPEAKERS, BENCHES, BLEACHERS, TRASH CANS, ETC. BLEACHERS IDENTIFIED TO BE RELOCATED WILL NEED TO BE SALVAGED AND PROTECTED BY CONTRACTOR AND
REINSTALLED PER THE LANDSCAPE PLANS.
6.
PRESERVE AND PROTECT EXISTING PLANTS, SYNTHETIC TURF, IRRIGATION, TRACK SURFACING AND HARDSCAPE THAT ARE TO REMAIN AND THAT ARE LOCATED OUTSIDE THE LIMITS
OF WORK. PRESERVE AND PROTECT EXISTING IRRIGATION PIPE, WIRES, AND SLEEVES NECESSARY FOR USE WITH EXISTING AND NEW IRRIGATION.  SEE TREE AND SHRUB
PRESERVATION SPECIFICATION, SECTION 32 01 90. SEE IRRIGATION PLANS.
7.
PRIOR TO BEGINNING WORK, CONTRACTOR TO INVENTORY EXISTING TREES AND MARK TREES PER SPECIFICATION SECTION 32 01 90, TREE AND SHRUB PRESERVATION.
CONTRACTOR TO VERIFY THAT EXISTING TREES TO REMAIN WILL NOT BE DAMAGED BY DEMOLITION OR CONSTRUCTION. IF EXISTING TREES AND/OR LANDSCAPE TO REMAIN ARE
DAMAGED DURING DEMOLITION OR CONSTRUCTION, THE DAMAGE AND/OR DEAD TREE(S)/SHRUB(S) TO BE REPLACED AT CONTRACTORS EXPENSE WITH PLANT(S) OF SAME GENUS,
SPECIES, VARIETY, FORM AND SIZE. EXISTING TREES WITH CALIPERS GREATER THAN 4" SHALL BE REPLACED WITH 60" BOX TREES.
8.
REMOVE AND SALVAGE AND RETURN ALL IRRIGATION VALVES, VALVE BOXES, CONTROLLERS, SPRAY HEADS AND ROTOR HEADS THAT ARE REMOVED, TO NYE COUNTY SCHOOL
DISTRICT (NCSD) UNLESS DIRECTED OTHERWISE BY THE A/E AND/OR NCSD/OWNER'S REPRESENTATIVE. SEE SPECIFICATIONS FOR SALVAGE PROCEDURES.
9.
PRESERVE, PROTECT AND/OR ALTER ALL IRRIGATION EQUIPMENT WITHIN EXISTING LANDSCAPE AREAS AS NECESSARY FOR A FINAL, COMPLETE AND FUNCTIONING IRRIGATION
SYSTEM NECESSARY FOR EXISTING PLANT MATERIAL TO REMAIN AND SYNTHETIC TURF. PROVIDE TEMPORARY IRRIGATION TO PREVENT UNINTERRUPTED IRRIGATION TO EXISTING
PLANT MATERIAL, IF NECESSARY. PROVIDE METHOD AND SCHEDULE OF TEMPORARY IRRIGATION FOR WRITTEN APPROVAL BY A/E. CONTRACTOR TO REMOVE IRRIGATION LINES
THAT ARE NO LONGER NECESSARY. SEE IRRIGATION PLANS FOR IRRIGATION DEMOLITION INFORMATION.
10. CONTRACTOR TO ENSURE THAT EXISTING TREES THAT ARE TO REMAIN GET ADEQUATE IRRIGATION.
11. CONTRACTOR SHALL PROTECT IN PLACE ALL EXISTING SLEEVES AND CHASES AT HARDSCAPE CROSSINGS.
12. IF NEW IRRIGATION IS REQUIRED THAT REQUIRES NEW SLEEVES CROSSING EXISTING ASPHALT AREAS,  A MINIMUM 3' WIDE PORTION OF ASPHALT SHALL BE SAW CUT AND REMOVED.
CUT LINES SHALL BE STRAIGHT AND PERPENDICULAR TO ADJACENT CURBS. ONCE IRRIGATION IS INSTALLED, WITH SLEEVES, CONTRACTOR SHALL REPLACE ASPHALT TO MATCH
EXISTING ASPHALT INSTALLATION.
13. CONTRACTOR TO FIELD VERIFY EXISTING IRRIGATION COMPONENTS' LOCATIONS PRIOR TO STARTING WORK AND THAT THE IRRIGATION IS FUNCTIONING PROPERLY. THIS INCLUDES
EXISTING IRRIGATION CONTROLLERS, BACKFLOW PREVENTER(S), MASTER VALVES AND FLOW SENSORS. CONTRACTOR TO BRING ANY MALFUNCTIONING COMPONENTS OR
PORTIONS OF THE IRRIGATION SYSTEM TO THE ATTENTION OF THE A/E.
14. CONTRACTOR TO REPLACE ANY EXISTING IRRIGATION COMPONENTS THAT ARE TO REMAIN AND DAMAGED DURING CONSTRUCTION WITH COMPONENTS OF THE SAME
MANUFACTURER, MODEL, SIZE, ETC. AT THE CONTRACTOR'S EXPENSE.
15. CONTRACTOR TO PROTECT IN PLACE ALL EXISTING HARDSCAPES, STRUCTURES, SCORE BOARDS, LIGHT POLES, FENCING, SITE FURNITURE AND OTHER APPURTENANCES THAT ARE
TO REMAIN. ELECTRICAL ACCESSORIES, WIRES AND CONDUITS TO LIGHTS, SCOREBOARD, AND OTHER POWERED AMENITIES SHALL BE PROTECTED IN PLACED. DAMAGE SHALL BE
REPAIRED BY THE CONTRACTOR AT HIS/HER EXPENSE.
16. CONTRACTOR TO REPAIR HARDSCAPE DAMAGED DURING CONSTRUCTION, THAT IS NOT TO BE REMOVED,  AT THE CONTRACTOR'S EXPENSE. REPLACED HARDSCAPE SHALL MATCH
THE EXISTING WITH MATERIALS, FINISHES, JOINTS, SCORING, ETC. DAMAGED HARDSCAPE PANELS SHALL BE REMOVED AND REPLACED IN THEIR ENTIRETY AND REPLACED TO THE
NEAREST JOINTS. DAMAGED ASPHALT SHALL BE REPLACED IN SECTIONS OF CONSISTENT WIDTH WITH A MINIMUM 3' WIDTH. HARDSCAPE INSTALLATION SHALL MATCH EXISTING
INSTALLATION, INCLUDING DEPTH, REINFORCEMENT AND BASE.
17. VERIFY THAT THE FIELD IS DRAINING PROPERLY PRIOR TO THE START OF DEMOLITION. DRAINAGE REVIEW AND POTENTIAL CORRECTIONS SHALL BE CONSIDERED AS PART OF THE
PROJECT AND OF THE PROJECT SCHEDULE, PRIOR TO THE START OF WORK. CONTRACTOR SHALL TEST THE PERCOLATION OF THE FIELD AREAS PRIOR TO THE INSTALLATION OF
NEW SYNTHETIC TURF. DIG A MINIMUM OF 8 HOLES AT THE FIELD. HOLES SHALL BE A MINIMUM 2'X2'X2' DEEP, SPACES THROUGHOUT THE FUTURE FIELDS' AREAS. FILL THE HOLES
WITH WATER. IF THE HOLES DO NOT PERCOLATE WITHIN 24 HOURS AND/OR IF THERE ARE ANY ISSUES OR INADEQUACIES, CONTACT THE A/E PRIOR TO PROVIDING A DEFERRED
DRAINAGE SUBMITTAL FOR EACH FIELD AREA. SEE DRAINAGE NOTES ON SHEET L1.00.
18. QUANTITIES ON THE PLANS ARE PROVIDED FOR REFERENCE ONLY. CONTRACTOR SHALL VERIFY ALL QUANTITIES. CONTRACTOR SHALL VERIFY THE QUANTITY OF EXISTING TREES
TO REMAIN AND TO BE DEMOLISHED PRIOR TO BID.
19. CONTRACTOR SHALL REMOVE ALL WEEDS IN LANDSCAPE PLANTER AREAS WITHIN THE LIMITS OF WORK. CONTRACTOR SHALL REMOVE ALL NEWLY GROWN / VOLUNTEER TREES
THAT HAVE GROWN FROM NATURAL SEED WITHIN THE AREAS WITHIN THE LIMIT OF WORK.
20. AVOID ALL UTILITIES AND OBSTRUCTIONS. CONTRACTOR TO LOCATE AND VERIFY ALL UTILITY LOCATIONS PRIOR TO CONSTRUCTION. CALL BEFORE YOU DIG AND CALL BEFORE YOU
OVER HEAD.
21. PROTECT IN PLACE ELECTRICAL BOXES AND OTHER UTILITY BOXES AND VAULTS TO REMAIN. ADJUST THE UTILITY BOX ELEVATIONS AS NEEDED TO BE TO MEET NEW FINISH
SURFACE GRADE.
22. CONTRACTOR TO USE CAUTION WHEN REMOVING EXISTING TURF AND IRRIGATION, ESPECIALLY IN CONSIDERATION OF EXISTING STRUCTURES, EXISTING IRRIGATION TO EXISTING
LANDSCAPE AND EXISTING TREE ROOT SYSTEMS. SOIL AT EXISTING TREE ROOT SYSTEMS WILL OFTEN NOT BE ABLE TO BE REMOVED TO THE DEPTH SPECIFIED. CONTRACTOR
SHALL USE SMALL TOOL TO REMOVE SOIL AT TREE ROOT SYSTEMS TO THE EXTENT POSSIBLE. SEE IRRIGATION PLANS FOR IRRIGATION DEMOLITION INFORMATION. SEE
SPECIFICATION SECTION 32 01 90, TREE AND SHRUB PRESERVATION.
23. DISPOSE OF ALL DEMOLISHED MATERIAL OFF SITE IN A LEGAL MANNER.
24. CONTRACTOR SHALL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR OBTAINING CONSTRUCTION PERMITS AS NEEDED FROM LOCAL AUTHORITIES. CONTRACTOR SHALL PAY ALL PERMIT FEES ASSOCIATED
WITH PERMITTING.
25. CONTRACTOR TO BRING ANY DISCREPANCIES TO THE ATTENTION OF THE A/E.
