## Known weakness this worksheet is looking for

Two independent spot-checks (A7-10, A10-30) found the recorded rationales **verbatim accurate** but **under-inclusive** — the agent captured each sheet's headline scope and skipped secondary trades visible on the same sheet:

- **A7-10** was assigned `ITB-054`/`RFP-031`, but the sheet also shows a stainless steel work table and tray slide (`ITB-085`), 2x wood framing / PT plywood / continuous nailer (`RFP-060`), and sealant (`ITB-040`).
- **A10-30** was assigned `ITB-071`/`ITB-077`, but its Finish Legend calls out FRP-1/FRP-2 fiber reinforced plastic panels and PT-2 accent paint (`RFP-060`, whose title explicitly includes FRP), plus keynote 9-03 wall base (possibly `ITB-066`, which has no spec section at all).

So the failure mode is **missed scope, not invented scope** — which is the safer direction, but it means a package can silently lose a drawing it should have inherited. Bias your checking toward *what else is on the sheet* rather than *is the stated claim true*.
