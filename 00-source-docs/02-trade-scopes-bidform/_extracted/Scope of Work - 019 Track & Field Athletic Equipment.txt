Scope of Work – Track & Field Athletic Equipment – Provide all materials, labor, equipment, and supervision for a complete scope of work per plans and specifications. This scope of work shall include, but not be limited to:
Supply and install National Federation of State High School Associations (NFHS) Compliant Track & Field Athletic Equipment with as specified, including:
Soccer goals, including netting and wheel assemblies.
Football goalposts (PE-102), including flags and padding.
Pole vault equipment (PE-105, PE-111).
Long / triple jump equipment (PE-103).
High jump, including landing system (PE-106).
Discus / shotput rings (PE-108, PE-109).
Discus cage (PE-107).
Include all structural components, welding, priming, prefinished items, and all associated components for a complete system.
Provide footings including excavation, backfill, compacted subbase, concrete, reinforcing, sleeves, anchors, coring if required, layout, and clean-up for these scopes of work.  Backfill all footing excavations.
Provide all anchors, fasteners, and sealants required to provide a complete scope of work.
Provide spare parts as required.
Provide final adjustments as required.
Supply and install Shot Put & Discuss Runway Mix (RS-105), including associated landscape edging (PE-110), as indicated and specified.
Supply and install Long / Triple Jump Pit Sand (RS-106) as indicated and specified.
Event Striping of track surfacing by others.
Materials and installation shall comply with applicable ADA requirement
Coordinate with Contractor for location of all underground piping prior to installation of foundations.
Comply with the requirements of the Geotechnical Report and Contract Documents for seismic requirements. In the event of a conflict in requirements between the documents, the most stringent requirement shall prevail.
The Project has a Class D Seismic Design Category; Seismic Importance Factor: 1.0.
Provide dust control for all operations related to this scope of work during working hours per Nye County Air Quality requirements.
Place all dirt spoils at a designated area on-site for removal by others.
Include multiple mobilizations as may be required per the Preliminary Construction Schedule and Logistics Plan.
