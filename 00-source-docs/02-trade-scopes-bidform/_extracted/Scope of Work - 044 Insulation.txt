Primary Specifications

Related Specifications
Division 00 – Procurement and Contracting Requirements
Division 01 – General Requirements

Scope of Work – Thermal & Sound Insulation – Provide all materials, labor, equipment, and supervision for a complete scope of work per plans and specifications. This scope of work shall include, but not be limited to:
Supply and install all interior and exterior board and fiberglass batt at walls, ceilings, and soffits, as indicated, including gaps at exterior doors, windows, and miscellaneous cavities.
Includes batt insulation beneath roof deck.
Includes exterior soffits, interior face of exterior (furred) walls, interior face of exterior framed walls.
Includes interior Z-furring as indicated and required.
Pin/attach batt insulation to wall above ceiling where gypsum wall board does not extend to deck, as indicated and required.
Provide insulation to meet specified thickness, R-value, Fire Rating, and STC Rating, as indicated and required.
Supply and install all acoustical (sound attenuation) insulation at interior partitions and ceilings as indicated, including gaps at top of acoustical walls, and acoustically rated door frames.
Includes all Restroom ceilings.
Supply insulation for door and window headers and jambs as indicated and required. Installation of same by others.
Supply and install poly seal at penetrations as indicated and required.
All materials shall comply with flame spread ratings as required; provide manufacturers certifications.
All materials shall comply with UL requirements as indicated and required.
Repair all related work at completion of project as needed prior to acceptance.
Include multiple mobilizations as may be required per the Preliminary Construction Schedule and Logistics Plan.
